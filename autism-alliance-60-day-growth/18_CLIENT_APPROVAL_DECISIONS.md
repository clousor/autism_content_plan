### Detailed Decision Briefs (Pre-Start):

1.  **Decision 1: 60-Day Growth Goal Approval**
    *   *Question for Client:* Do you approve the Target Growth Trajectory of **+6,500 qualified Instagram followers**, 1.8M+ impressions, and 150–210 direct parent inquiries across Delhi NCR and Ludhiana?
    *   *Impact:* Anchors all weekly sprint targets and reporting metrics.
2.  **Decision 2: 60-Day Media Budget Approval**
    *   *Question for Client:* Do you confirm the Recommended Media Budget of **₹3,00,000** over 60 days (billed directly to Meta and Google Ads managers at ~₹5,000/day)?
    *   *Impact:* Dictates cold awareness reach, local geo-fencing radius, and retargeting capacity.
3.  **Decision 3: Delhi NCR / Faridabad Commercial Priority**
    *   *Question for Client:* Do you approve allocating 75% of paid advertising and local franchise content to Delhi East of Kailash and Faridabad Crown Plaza, with 25% dedicated to Ludhiana?
    *   *Rationale:* Delhi NCR represents high market density and urgent parent demand; Ludhiana already enjoys established local authority.
4.  **Decision 4: Dr. Atul Madaan Batch Filming Schedule**
    *   *Question for Client:* Can Dr. Atul commit to two 3.5-hour batch filming windows per month (e.g., the 1st and 3rd Wednesday mornings) to record 16–20 Reels and 2 YouTube masterclasses?
    *   *Impact:* Guarantees content pipeline stability without interrupting daily patient appointments.
5.  **Decision 5: Centre Filming Permissions & Staff Availability**
    *   *Question for Client:* Do our videographers have authorization to film in the sensory gyms and speech cabins in East of Kailash and Crown Plaza Faridabad between 8:30 AM and 11:30 AM, with 1–2 therapists demonstrating activities on camera?
    *   *Impact:* Secures vital hands-on demonstration footage that drives saves and shares.
6.  **Decision 6: Child Privacy & Consent Protocol Sign-Off**
    *   *Question for Client:* Do you formally adopt our *Child Dignity and Privacy Protocol*, mandating that 90% of footage focuses on therapists/tools and zero minor faces are published without formal written guardian consent?
    *   *Impact:* Absolute legal and clinical compliance under RCI, NMC, and DPDP regulations.
7.  **Decision 7: Central WhatsApp / Inquiry Handling Assignment**
    *   *Question for Client:* Who at the clinic reception is designated as the primary responder for inbound WhatsApp and telephone inquiries generated by the campaign?
    *   *SLA Standard:* Incoming parent messages must receive a compassionate, structured response within 15–30 minutes during business hours.
8.  **Decision 8: One Central Social Presence**
    *   *Question for Client:* Do you approve maintaining one unified Instagram and YouTube channel under the co-branded banner *"Dr. Atul Madaan | Autism Alliance"*, supported by 3 distinct Google Business Profiles?
    *   *Impact:* Prevents audience fragmentation and maximizes algorithmic velocity.
