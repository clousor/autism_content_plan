## 1. Regional Market Dynamics: Why Ludhiana is Unique

While Delhi NCR is an acquisition market where Autism Alliance is scaling rapid digital brand recognition, **Ludhiana is an established authority fortress**. Dr. Atul Madaan’s clinical work has deep roots across Punjab, with families historically traveling from Jalandhar, Amritsar, Bathinda, Khanna, and Patiala to seek his diagnostic guidance.

### Key Cultural & Behavioral Insights in Punjab:
1.  **High Respect for Established Heritage:** In Punjab, healthcare decisions are driven heavily by family elders, word-of-mouth recommendations, and direct clinical doctor reputation rather than flashy marketing.
2.  **Joint Family Dynamics:** Grandparents and extended family members frequently attend therapy consultations and evaluations. Content must empower parents with respectful arguments to address elder skepticism (*"Bachhe nu thoda time do, aape bol pau"*).
3.  **Aspiration for Metro-Quality Care Locally:** Punjab families dread having to travel to Delhi or PGIMER Chandigarh every week for therapy. Positioning Autism Alliance Ludhiana as **offering world-class South Delhi clinical standards right in Kochar Market** is an unbeatable value proposition.
