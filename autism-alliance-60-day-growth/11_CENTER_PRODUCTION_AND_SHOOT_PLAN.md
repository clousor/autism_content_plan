## 1. The Production Philosophy: "Batch Once, Distribute for 30 Days"

Daily ad-hoc filming disrupts clinical schedules, exhausts therapists, and produces inconsistent video quality. Autism Alliance will operate an industrial **batch-production model** where structured half-day shoots yield 3–4 weeks of high-definition video assets, b-roll banks, and photography.

*   **Timing:** 08:30 AM – 11:30 AM (strictly before full afternoon patient traffic).

## 3. The Comprehensive Centre Filming Checklist

Every shoot must systematically capture assets across three categories:

### Category A: Facility & Environmental B-Roll
*   [ ] **Exterior Establishing Shot:** Main road perspective showing signage clearly (e.g., C-3 East of Kailash Main Road, Crown Plaza Mall Sector 15A facade).
*   [ ] **Entrance & Reception:** Clean glass doors opening, branded Autism Alliance logo, welcoming receptionist smiling, visitor sign-in register.
*   [ ] **Waiting Lounge:** Comfortable seating, clean water dispenser, child books and non-electronic wooden puzzles on table.
*   [ ] **Sensory Gym Wide Shots:** Bolster swings, platform swings, hammock swings, crash pads, climbing ladder, tactile stepping stones, balance beams.
*   [ ] **Speech Therapy Cabins:** Sound-treated partition, table and chair set at child eye-level, mirror on wall for oral motor therapy.
*   [ ] **Sanitization Protocols:** Therapist wiping down therapy swing and wooden toys with disinfectant wipes (critical trust builder for mothers).

### Category B: Clinical Staff & Human Elements
*   [ ] **Therapist Morning Prep:** Therapist entering room, switching on soft sensory lights, setting out colorful activity bins.
*   [ ] **Case Planning:** Dr. Atul Madaan and an Occupational Therapist reviewing an individualized treatment folder and discussing with respectful expressions.
*   [ ] **Direct-to-Camera Explanations:** Therapist standing in front of therapy equipment delivering 15-second soundbites.
*   [ ] **Warm Welcome:** Receptionist greeting a family with hands folded (*"Namaste"*) or warm handshake.

### Category C: Macro Activity & Hands-Only B-Roll (Zero Minor Exposure)
*   [ ] **Fine Motor Tasks:** Close-up of adult and child hands (or simulated hands) picking up colored beads, sorting buttons, using wooden tongs.
*   [ ] **Sensory Bins:** Hands plunging into rice/rajma sensory bins, burying and finding hidden plastic animals.
*   [ ] **Blowing & Oral Motor:** Therapist blowing bubbles, whistles, pinwheels.
*   [ ] **Proprioceptive Heavy Work:** Rolling on a large peanut ball, carrying a weighted animal cushion.
*   [ ] **Visual Communication:** Fingers pointing to PECS cards: "Eat", "Play", "Stop", "Water".

## 4. Equipment Standards & Technical Presets

High production quality does not require ₹10-lakh cinema rigs; it requires **immaculate audio, stable framing, and flattering lighting**.

### Recommended Equipment Kit (Total Investment: ~₹45,000 – ₹70,000):
1.  **Primary Camera / Smartphone:** iPhone 15 Pro / 16 Pro (or Samsung S24 Ultra) shooting in 4K at 30fps (or 60fps for B-roll slow motion).
2.  **Audio (Non-Negotiable):** DJI Mic 2 or Rode Wireless PRO (dual-channel lavalier system with windscreens). Clear, crisp, noise-isolated audio is mandatory.
3.  **Lighting:** 1x Godox SL-60W (or Amaran 100d) with a 90cm octagonal softbox diffuser for key light; 1x portable bi-color LED wand for subtle rim/hair light.
4.  **Stabilization:** DJI Osmo Mobile 6 (gimbal for smooth walking tours) + heavy-duty aluminum fluid-head tripod for static talking head.
5.  **Teleprompter / Script Tablet:** Small iPad / tablet mounted on tripod rig using the *PromptSmart Pro* app for Dr. Atul's technical clinical terms.

## 5. Subtitle & Framing Safe Zones (Meta & YouTube 2026)

To prevent captions or key hooks from being covered by platform UI elements:
*   **Top 15% Safe Area:** Keep free of essential text (reserved for username, audio tag).
*   **Bottom 20% Safe Area:** Keep free of essential text (reserved for Reel caption, audio title, comment box).
*   **Right Edge 15%:** Keep free of text (reserved for Like, Comment, Share icons).
*   **Center 65% Sweet Spot:** All primary captions, bold headlines, and demonstration action must occur within the central 1080x1350 pixel zone.
*   **Subtitle Styling:** Font: *Montserrat Bold* or *Inter Bold*; yellow/white color with slight black drop-shadow; 1 to 3 words highlighted synchronously with spoken speech (karaoke effect).

## 6. Child Protection, Dignity & Written Consent Protocol

Autism Alliance operates under strict clinical ethics and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act:

1.  **Guiding Principle:** **Minor anonymity is the default.** 90% of all published footage will feature therapists, facilities, materials, and hands. No identifiable child face is ever required to demonstrate high-value clinical therapy.
2.  **Mandatory Written Consent:** If a parent explicitly requests or consents to their child being featured in an inspirational progress story, a formal signed *Autism Alliance Media Authorization Form* must be archived physically and digitally prior to filming.
3.  **Prohibited Footage (Zero-Tolerance):**
    *   Never film a child experiencing an active meltdown, emotional distress, or crying.
    *   Never film a child in any state of undress or during bathroom/toileting activities.
    *   Never display diagnostic test score sheets with identifiable child names or family contact details.
    *   Never display school uniforms or school identification badges.
