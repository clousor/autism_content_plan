# Autism Alliance — 60-Day Interactive Live Broadcast Strategy

**Document Reference:** Bi-Weekly Live Streaming & Cross-Platform Webinar Program  
**Host:** Dr. Atul Madaan, RCI Regd. Clinical Psychologist  
**Cadence:** 1 Live Every 2 Weeks (4 Flagship Collaborative Broadcasts over 60 Days)  
**Primary Broadcast Channel:** Instagram Live (Dual-Host Split Screen) simulcast to YouTube Live and Facebook Live  

---

## 1. Live Strategy & Platform Mechanics in 2026

### Collaboration Lives vs. Recorded Expert Conversations:
*   *Platform Intelligence 2026:* A dual-host Instagram Live automatically notifies the followers of **both** accounts. When Dr. Atul co-hosts a Live with an influential pediatrician or parenting creator, Autism Alliance gains immediate, live access to an external, highly targeted parent audience.
*   *Medical Compliance Guardrail:* **Never diagnose a child live on air.** Every Live broadcast will feature a mandatory verbal disclaimer and pinned comment:
    > *"Disclaimer: This broadcast is for general educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice or clinical diagnosis. For personalized evaluation, consult an RCI-registered professional."*

### LIVE SESSION 1 (Week 2): "Early Signs vs. Overthinking: What Parents in Delhi NCR Need to Know"
*   **Target Date:** Day 11 (Thursday Evening, 8:00 PM – 8:45 PM IST)
*   **Host:** Dr. Atul Madaan (Managing Director, Autism Alliance)
*   **Co-Host / Guest:** Prominent South Delhi Pediatrician (e.g., Senior Consultant Pediatrician, Apollo Cradle / Rainbow Children's Hospital South Delhi network)
*   **Duration:** 45 minutes
*   **Audience Target:** Parents of children aged 12 to 36 months observing delayed milestones.
*   **Run of Show & Agenda:**
    *   `00:00 – 05:00` | **Opening & Welcome:** Dr. Atul and Guest welcome parents; address the common fear: *"Am I overthinking my child's delay?"*
    *   `05:00 – 18:00` | **Clinical Discussion:** Pediatrician explains what they look for in an OPD wellness check (physical milestones vs developmental milestones); Dr. Atul explains red flags (eye contact, name response, pointing).
    *   `18:00 – 30:00` | **De-stigmatization & Family Myths:** Addressing common advice from Indian family elders (*"Wait till age 3, boys speak late"*).
    *   `30:00 – 42:00` | **Moderated Audience Q&A:** Selecting filtered, non-diagnostic parent questions from the question sticker.
    *   `42:00 – 45:00` | **Closing & Next Steps:** Announcement of free downloadable *18-Month Observation Checklist*.
*   **Pre-Screened Live Questions:**
    1.  *"My 20-month-old responds to TV tunes but not his name. Is this hearing or developmental?"*
    2.  *"Doctor, when should a parent see a pediatrician vs directly seeing a child psychologist?"*
    3.  *"How does early therapy actually change brain development before age 3?"*
