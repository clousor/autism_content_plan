## 4. YouTube Audit (`@dratulmadaan_autismexpert`)

### Current Performance Dynamics:
*   **Search Intent vs. Browse Traffic:** Long-form videos perform exceptionally well when aligned with evergreen diagnostic queries (*"speech delay vs autism"*, *"ABC model of behavior"*). Watch time retention is strong due to Dr. Atul's detailed breakdowns.
*   **The Shorts Gap:** Shorts currently represent repurposed clips without dedicated 9:16 vertical formatting or bold on-screen headlines, resulting in lower YouTube Shorts feed velocity.
*   **Thumbnail & Packaging:** Long-form thumbnails require modern YouTube medical formatting: clear facial expression, 3-word high-contrast text, and illustrative visual cue (e.g., child playing vs. child isolated).

### Strategic Adjustments:
1.  **Publish 1 Flagship Long-Form Video Every Week (8 Master Videos over 60 Days):** Focused on high-intent search keywords (8–14 minutes duration).
2.  **The 1-to-6 Repurposing Engine:** Each master video systematically generates 4–6 high-impact Shorts with custom hooks, driving viewers back to the full-length video.
3.  **Search Optimization:** Add rich descriptions, clinical timestamps, and pinned comments directing viewers to the Autism Alliance consultation helpline.

## 5. Facebook Audit (`dratulmadaanautismexpert`)

### Platform Realities in Delhi NCR & Punjab:
*   Facebook in India has a demographic skew toward parents aged 32–50, extended family members, grandparents, and tier-2/tier-3 city residents.
*   In families suspecting developmental delays, grandparents and aunts often play a critical role in either denying the delay or urging parents to seek help.
*   *Actionable Playbook:*
    *   Upload all Reels **natively** to Facebook (never auto-share a low-res Instagram mirror).
    *   Write longer, compassionate narrative captions in Hindi/Hinglish explaining common family dilemmas (*"Jab parivaar kahe ki 'ladke der se bolte hain', tab parents kya karein?"*).
