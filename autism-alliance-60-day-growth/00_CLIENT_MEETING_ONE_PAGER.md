### 4. The 3 Winning Content Franchises (What We Will Become Famous For)
If Autism Alliance could only be known for three recurring formats over the next 60 days, they will be:

1.  **"AA | Parent Asked. Therapist Shows" (Short-Form Demonstration)**
    *   *Concept:* 25–35 second practical Reels showing an OT or Speech Therapist explaining what an everyday exercise actually achieves (e.g., *"Aapko lag raha hai ye sirf ek ball game hai? Therapist actually sensory processing aur motor planning build kar raha hai."*).
    *   *Why it wins:* High save and share rate; eliminates parent mystery; showcases real facilities.
2.  **"AA | Dr. Atul Explains" (De-mystifying Early Signs & Parental Guilt)**
    *   *Concept:* 30–45 second pattern-interrupt doctor explainer confronting parent anxieties (e.g., *"Speech delay vs Autism: 3 clear observations parents miss at 18 months"*).
    *   *Why it wins:* Positions Dr. Atul as India's most empathetic, authoritative clinical psychologist.
3.  **"AA | Inside The Centre / Real Progress" (Humanized BTS & Environment)**
    *   *Concept:* 20–30 second sensory tours, therapist prep routines, and multi-skill activity breakdowns across East of Kailash, Faridabad, and Ludhiana.
    *   *Why it wins:* Reduces parent activation energy and fear of visiting a physical clinic.
