## PART 1: THE 8-WEEK DAILY STORY OPERATING SYSTEM

Stories build intimacy, community retention, and daily mental availability. An account that only posts Reels feels like an automated broadcast; an account with a dynamic Story presence feels like a **living, breathing human organization**.

### The Golden Rule of Stories:
> **Stories must NEVER consist solely of reposting feed Reels.** Reposted Reels get under 3% viewer retention. Stories must feature native stickers, polls, BTS glimpses, and direct questions.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       DAILY 4-STORY CADENCE BLUEPRINT                   │
├───────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIME  │ FORMAT TYPE          │ OBJECTIVE & CONTENT                      │
├───────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 09:00 │ Morning BTS / Centre │ Welcoming atmosphere; sensory gym setup; │
│       │ Atmosphere           │ coffee/prep with therapist quote.        │
├───────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 13:00 │ Interactive Sticker  │ Poll, Quiz, or Slider asking parents     │
│       │ (Poll / Quiz)        │ about everyday toddler behaviors.        │
├───────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 17:00 │ Micro-Education Tip  │ 1 practical home hack or Reel teaser     │
│       │ / Reel Hook          │ linking to the daily 5:30 PM Reel.       │
├───────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 20:30 │ Q&A / Community      │ Answering 1 direct parent question or    │
│       │ Reassurance          │ gentle supportive quote from Dr. Atul.   │
└───────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Recurring Weekly Story Templates

*   **Monday: "Morning at the Centre & Motivation"**
    *   *Story 1:* Video pan across the clean, softly lit sensory gym at East of Kailash or Faridabad.
    *   *Story 2:* Quote card: *"Reminder for the week: Progress in therapy isn't measured in leaps, it's measured in micro-moments. You are doing enough."*
    *   *Story 3:* Poll Sticker: *"Did your child struggle with sleep or routines over the weekend? [Yes / Manageable]"*
*   **Tuesday: "Ask the Therapist (Micro-Demo)"**
    *   *Story 1:* Senior Speech Pathologist holding a toy: *"Quick speech hack: Instead of asking 'What color is this?', try saying 'Look, a blue car!' and pause."*
    *   *Story 2:* Emoji Slider: *"How often do you find yourself quizzing your toddler?"*
*   **Wednesday: "Clinical Myth Buster Quiz"**
    *   *Story 1:* Multiple Choice Quiz Sticker: *"Can excessive screen time cause Autism Spectrum Disorder? [A. Yes, directly | B. No, but causes virtual delay | C. Autism is contagious]"*
    *   *Story 2:* Dr. Atul 15-second video explaining the correct answer (B).
*   **Thursday: "Live Countdown & Question Drop"**
    *   *Story 1:* Countdown Sticker for upcoming Live session / Weekend Masterclass.
    *   *Story 2:* Question Sticker: *"Drop your question on [Topic] here for Dr. Atul to answer tonight!"*
*   **Friday: "Behind-the-Scenes & Team Humanizing"**
    *   *Story 1:* Photo of the multidisciplinary team in Faridabad or Delhi during their weekly case-coordination meeting.
    *   *Story 2:* *"Meet Pooja, our Senior Occupational Therapist. Her superpower? Turning sensory regulation into fun games for kids."*
*   **Saturday: "Centre Open Day & Walk-In Reminder"**
    *   *Story 1:* Location tag & exterior photo: *"Open today till 6:00 PM at C-3 East of Kailash & LG-06 Crown Plaza Faridabad."*
    *   *Story 2:* Direct Call / WhatsApp link sticker for parents wanting a facility tour.
*   **Sunday: "Dr. Atul Parent Q&A Box"**
    *   *Stories 1–4:* Dr. Atul answering 3 top questions submitted by parents over the weekend.
