# Autism Alliance — Child Privacy, Protection & Clinical Ethics Policy

**Document Reference:** Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) on Child Dignity, Guardian Consent & Healthcare Compliance  
**Legal Frameworks:** Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) Act 1992, Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR) Guidelines  
**Guiding Mandate:** **The psychological dignity, physical safety, and long-term digital privacy of every child supersede all marketing and content objectives.**  

---

## 1. The Core Creative Standard: "Hands, Tools & Environments First"

To eliminate the ethical dilemmas and risks of filming minors, Autism Alliance operates under an unambiguous default standard:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    CONTENT PRIVACY PYRAMID (90/10 RULE)                 │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 90% OF ALL PUBLISHED CONTENT (DEFAULT ZERO CHILD EXPOSURE):             │
│ • Professional therapist explanations (Face to Camera)                  │
│ • Macro B-roll of Adult Therapist Hands demonstrating activities        │
│ • Close-ups of physical therapy tools, sensory swings, pegboards, PECS  │
│ • Beautiful cinematic shots of empty, sanitized therapy rooms           │
│ • Graphic animations, milestone tables, and decision-tree carousels     │
│ • Staged demonstrations where adults simulate child motor actions       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 10% MAXIMUM EXCEPTION (STRICTLY WITH FORMAL WRITTEN GUARDIAN CONSENT):  │
│ • Inspirational, positive parent journey vignettes                      │
│ • Filmed from behind (back of head / over-the-shoulder only)            │
│ • Zero identifiable minor facial features or distress moments           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Content Practices (Zero-Tolerance Blacklist)

Any staff member or videographer violating these guidelines will be subject to immediate disciplinary action and removal from production:

1.  **NO Filming of Meltdowns or Distress:** Never film a child crying, screaming, self-harming, or experiencing sensory dysregulation for "viral engagement". Exploiting a minor's neurological panic is an ethical violation.
2.  **NO Remote Diagnostic Labeling:** Never point camera at a specific child and state *"This child has severe autism"*.
3.  **NO Identifiable Personal Data:**
    *   Never display diagnostic assessment score sheets (CARS, ADOS, Vineland).
    *   Never disclose child names, age, address, phone numbers, or city of residence.
    *   Never film school uniforms, school bags, logos, or ID cards.
4.  **NO Sensational "Before and After" Cures:** Never publish videos claiming a child was "cured" or "transformed in 30 days" through therapy.
5.  **NO Filming in Restrooms:** All toileting, hygiene, and diaper-changing activities are strictly off-limits to cameras.

## 4. Digital Asset Storage & Security SOP

1.  **Encrypted Local Archiving:** All raw footage captured at the centres must be transferred immediately to a secure, password-protected, encrypted drive accessible only by the Lead Videographer and Growth Lead.
2.  **Raw Footage Deletion:** Any incidental footage that accidentally captured identifiable patients in corridors or waiting rooms must be permanently purged within 24 hours.
3.  **Third-Party Agency Non-Disclosure:** All external video editors and production contractors must sign a binding Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) legally preventing the retention, copying, or secondary distribution of any clinic footage.
