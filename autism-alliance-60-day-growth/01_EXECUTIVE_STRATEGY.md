## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objective

The commercial and clinical imperative for Autism Alliance over the next 60 days is **to become the most trusted, recognized, and visible child development brand in Delhi NCR**, while sustaining its regional leadership in Ludhiana.

Rather than running generic corporate healthcare ads or publishing static graphics that parents scroll past, Autism Alliance will deploy an **evidence-based organic content flywheel backed by precision paid amplification**. This system solves the fundamental psychological friction parents experience when facing developmental concerns: **uncertainty, fear of judgment, and confusion over therapies**.

By combining Dr. Atul Madaan’s clinical authority with transparent, behind-the-scenes therapist demonstrations across the three centres, Autism Alliance will build **mental availability**, driving organic follower compounding and high-intent consultation inquiries.

## 4. Account Architecture Recommendation: One Central Brand vs. Separate Pages

A core question for multi-location healthcare practices is whether to run separate social handles for each clinic or a centralized hub.

### The Analysis

| Factor | Option A: One Central Brand + Location Franchises (Recommended) | Option B: 3 Independent Social Accounts (Not Recommended) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Algorithmic Strength** | **Extremely High.** Concentrates all views, saves, shares, and watch time into one authority profile, triggering Explore page momentum. | **Severely Diluted.** Views are split across 3 weak accounts; none achieve enough algorithmic velocity to break out. |
| **Production Overhead** | **Sustainable.** 1 cohesive content calendar produced in centralized batches. | **Crippling.** Requires producing 3 separate daily posting schedules (15–21 posts/week). |
| **Clinical Leadership** | **Maximizes Dr. Atul Madaan’s Personal Brand.** Dr. Atul acts as the unified thought leader across all regions. | **Confusing.** Which account does Dr. Atul represent? Causes audience friction. |
| **Paid Media Efficiency** | **High.** All pixel data, engagement audiences, and social proof accumulate on one handle. Ad spend is concentrated. | **Inefficient.** Minimum budgets split three ways; learning phase reset on each account. |
| **Local Discovery Solution** | Handled via **3 distinct Google Business Profiles (GBPs)**, location tags, localized Reel series (`AA | NCR`), and geo-radius ads. | Relies on Instagram search for location, which accounts for <2% of clinical inquiries. |

### Final Architectural Recommendation:
1.  **Maintain ONE Centralized Social Presence:**
    *   **Instagram:** `@dratul_autismexpert` (Display name optimized to: *Dr. Atul Madaan | Autism Alliance*).
    *   **YouTube:** `@dratulmadaan_autismexpert` (Channel branding updated to: *Autism Alliance by Dr. Atul Madaan*).
    *   **Facebook:** Unified Autism Alliance / Dr. Atul Madaan page with localized address tags.
2.  **Establish Three Independent, Optimized Google Business Profiles:**
    *   *Autism Alliance — Child Development & Autism Centre (East of Kailash, Delhi)*
    *   *Autism Alliance — Child Development & Autism Centre (Crown Plaza Mall, Sector 15A, Faridabad)*
    *   *Autism Alliance — Child Development & Autism Centre (Kochar Market Road, Ludhiana)*
3.  **Local Franchise Content Integration:**
    *   Use dedicated Instagram Story Highlights for each centre: `Delhi Centre`, `Faridabad`, `Ludhiana`.
    *   Include clear visual on-screen tags on every Reel indicating which centre is featured (e.g., *"Filmed at Autism Alliance Faridabad Centre"*).

## 5. Strategic Answers to the Four Core Questions

### Q1: If Autism Alliance could become famous for only THREE recurring content formats during the next 60 days, what should they be and why?

1.  **Format 1: "Parent Asked. Therapist Shows" (Demonstration / Practical Solution)**
    *   *Structure:* 25–35 second Reel. Hook opens with a parent's real frustration or confusion. The therapist physically shows the activity on camera using real toys/materials and explains the hidden skill being developed.
    *   *Why:* Indian parents are inundated with theoretical advice. Demonstrating real therapy demystifies the black box of OT and speech therapy, driving massive save and share rates among parent WhatsApp groups.
2.  **Format 2: "Dr. Atul Explains: Before You Assume" (Diagnostic Clarification)**
    *   *Structure:* 30–45 second doctor-talking-head with dynamic B-roll. Breaks down subtle distinctions (e.g., *Speech delay vs Autism*, *Tantrum vs Meltdown*, *Sensory overload vs Stubbornness*).
    *   *Why:* Tackles the exact search queries and anxious late-night thoughts of parents. Establishes Dr. Atul as the definitive, calm clinical authority.
3.  **Format 3: "Inside The Centre: A Day in Therapy" (Environmental Familiarity & BTS)**
    *   *Structure:* 20–30 second aesthetic walkthrough showing sensory rooms, speech therapy toolkits, and therapist morning prep at East of Kailash, Faridabad, or Ludhiana.
    *   *Why:* Dismantles the stigma and clinical intimidation of taking a child to an assessment center. Shows that the centres are joyful, welcoming, and scientifically equipped.

### Q2: If we could spend advertising money on only THREE types of content, what should they be?

1.  **Ad Type 1: Organic High-Share Winner Amplification (Cold Awareness)**
    *   *Format:* Taking the top 10% performing educational Reels (those with verified high share-to-reach ratios) and running them as Meta Advantage+ video reach ads targeted strictly within an 8km radius of Delhi EOK and 10km radius of Faridabad Sector 15A.
2.  **Ad Type 2: "First Time Visiting a Child Development Centre?" (Local Consideration & Reassurance)**
    *   *Format:* A warm, 45-second guided video tour of the East of Kailash or Faridabad facility led by the Centre Manager or a Senior Therapist, explaining what happens during an initial developmental consultation.
3.  **Ad Type 3: "Parent Milestones Checklist" Lead Magnet (High-Intent Conversion)**
    *   *Format:* Carousel or Short Video promoting a downloadable *12-to-36 Month Child Development Observation Checklist*, driving parents directly to WhatsApp conversation with the clinical intake coordinator.

### Q3: What should we STOP posting immediately?
*   **STOP Canva quote cards with stock graphics:** Text posters of developmental quotes get zero algorithmic reach on Instagram in 2026.
*   **STOP generic festival wish flyers:** Publishing generic "Happy Diwali" or "World Health Day" flyers with clinic logos clutters the feed and lowers algorithmic engagement.
*   **STOP fear-inducing or judgmental captions:** Never use copy like *"Are you ignoring your child's warning signs?"* or implying that parents caused developmental delays through screen time.
*   **STOP unstructured talking videos filmed on low-quality webcams:** Blurry, poorly lit videos without subtitles or sound hooks destroy credibility.

### Q4: What should we DOUBLE DOWN on?
*   **DOUBLE DOWN on hands-and-materials B-roll:** High-definition close-up footage of sensory swings, textured toys, pegboards, and PECS communication cards being used by therapists.
*   **DOUBLE DOWN on Dr. Atul’s pattern-interrupt hooks:** Opening videos with counter-intuitive, compassionate truths (e.g., *"Stimming ko rokna bachhe ke liye harmful ho sakta hai. Yahan samajhiye kyun."*).
*   **DOUBLE DOWN on regional tagging and landmark references:** Explicitly mentioning *"Our East of Kailash centre in South Delhi"* and *"Our Sector 15A centre in Crown Plaza Faridabad"* in spoken video audio and captions.
*   **DOUBLE DOWN on WhatsApp conversation starters:** Directing viewers to send a specific trigger word (e.g., *"Comment or DM 'CHECKLIST' for the observation guide"*) to initiate warm, helpful parent dialogues.
