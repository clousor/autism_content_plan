## 1. 2026 Paid Media Landscape & Regulatory Compliance

### A. Meta Healthcare Advertising Regulations in 2026
*   **Prohibition of Sensitive Attribute Targeting:** Meta strictly forbids targeting users based on health conditions, medical history, or perceived disabilities. Advertisers cannot target "Autism", "Asperger's", "Speech Delay", or "ADHD" as user traits.
*   **Compliant Targeting Strategy (Proxy & Demographic Stacking):**
    1.  *Demographic Filters:* Parents with Toddlers (1–2 years), Parents with Preschoolers (3–5 years), Parents with Early School Children (6–8 years).
    2.  *Interest Proxies:* Early Childhood Education, Child Development, Pediatric Healthcare, Montessori Education, Sensory Play, Speech-Language Pathology.
    3.  *Geo-Fencing:* Tight geographic radius targeting around physical centres.

### B. Meta 2026 Reels Ad Formats
*   **Instagram & Facebook Reels Video Views / Engagement:** 9:16 vertical full-screen video ads optimized for **ThruPlay (15-second completed views)** and **Profile Visits**.
*   **Advantage+ Creative:** Allowing Meta’s AI engine to dynamically deliver the best creative variation to users most likely to engage or message on WhatsApp.

### C. Google Ads & Healthcare Policies
*   Google Ads prohibits remarketing based on sensitive health categories. However, **Google Search Ads on high-intent localized search queries** (*"autism centre near me"*, *"speech therapist South Delhi"*) are 100% compliant and convert at industry-leading rates.

## 5. The Organic-to-Paid Winner System (The 24–48 Hour Rule)

Autism Alliance will **never spend media budget on untested creative**. Every video must first prove itself in the organic arena before receiving paid amplification.

### Evaluation Metrics Evaluated at Hour 24 & Hour 48:
*   **Metric 1: Share-to-Reach Ratio (Sends per Reach):** The purest indicator of high emotional resonance.
*   **Metric 2: Save-to-Reach Ratio:** The indicator of future reference utility.
*   **Metric 3: 3-Second Hold Rate:** Audience retention velocity.
*   **Metric 4: Follows per 1,000 Views:** Follower conversion efficiency.

### The Decision Matrix:

```
                      ORGANIC PERFORMANCE SCORING (Hour 24-48)
┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
```
