## 2. Answers to the Four Core Strategic Mandates

### 1. If Autism Alliance could become famous for only THREE recurring content formats during the next 60 days, what should those formats be and why?
*   **Format 1: "AA | Parent Asked. Therapist Shows" (Short-Form Demonstration):**
    *   *Why:* In pediatric healthcare, parents are exhausted by abstract lectures. Demonstrating real therapy (e.g., using bubble wands for eye contact, platform swings for vestibular regulation, or kitchen beans for pincer grasp) completely de-mystifies the clinic experience. It drives the highest **Saves and Shares** on Instagram, directly triggering algorithm recommendations to other parents.
*   **Format 2: "AA | Dr. Atul Explains: Before You Assume" (Diagnostic Clarification):**
    *   *Why:* Dr. Atul Madaan’s clinical stature (RCI Registered Clinical Psychologist, CRR No. A56122) is our greatest competitive moat. By confronting common misconceptions (*"Speech Delay vs Autism"*, *"Why Boys Don't Just Talk Late"*, *"Never Stop Stimming"*), he positions Autism Alliance as India's most empathetic, scientifically grounded clinical voice.
*   **Format 3: "AA | Inside The Centre / Real Work" (Environmental Familiarity):**
    *   *Why:* Stepping into a child development centre is emotionally terrifying for anxious parents. Cinematic, warm walkthroughs of our East of Kailash sensory gym and Crown Plaza Faridabad facility normalize the clinic, dismantle stigma, and drastically reduce the friction required for a family to book a walk-in visit.

### 2. If we could spend advertising money on only THREE types of content, what should they be?
*   **Ad Type 1: Organic Winner Amplification (Top-of-Funnel Cold Reach):**
    *   *Mechanism:* Taking only the top 10% organically proven educational Reels (those achieving >3.0% share-to-reach ratio) and running them as Advantage+ video ads targeted strictly within an 8km radius of Delhi EOK and a 10km radius of Faridabad Crown Plaza.
*   **Ad Type 2: Local Centre Video Walkthroughs (Mid-Funnel Trust & Proximity):**
    *   *Mechanism:* 30-second guided tours showing the clean, colorful sensory rooms, friendly reception, and easy parking in Sector 15A Faridabad and East of Kailash, paired with a "Get Directions" / "Learn More" call to action.
*   **Ad Type 3: "12–36 Month Parent Observation Checklist" (Bottom-of-Funnel Conversion):**
    *   *Mechanism:* High-value lead magnet ads offering our free developmental observation rubric, driving high-intent parents directly into automated, supportive WhatsApp conversations with our clinic intake coordinator.

### 3. What should we STOP posting immediately?
*   **STOP Canva quote cards with stock images:** They convey zero medical authenticity and receive near-zero algorithmic distribution in 2026.
*   **STOP generic festival flyers:** "Happy Diwali" posters with clinic phone numbers clutter the grid and lower account engagement.
*   **STOP fear-inducing or shaming hooks:** Copy implying parents damaged their child through screen time damages trust and violates healthcare ethics.
*   **STOP unedited, low-quality smartphone clips:** Poor audio and dim lighting undermine clinical legitimacy.

### 4. What should we DOUBLE DOWN on?
*   **DOUBLE DOWN on hands-and-materials macro B-roll:** Showing adult therapist hands engaging with real sensory tools and communicative toys.
*   **DOUBLE DOWN on pattern-interrupt compassionate hooks:** Opening videos by validating parent anxiety and offering immediate, calm clarity.
*   **DOUBLE DOWN on hyper-local geographic tagging:** Explicitly mentioning *"Our East of Kailash centre in South Delhi"* and *"Our Sector 15A centre in Crown Plaza Faridabad"* across audio, captions, and on-screen graphics.
*   **DOUBLE DOWN on WhatsApp conversation starters:** Using automated DM keywords (e.g., *"DM 'CHECKLIST'"*) to bridge the gap between social content and direct clinical appointments.
