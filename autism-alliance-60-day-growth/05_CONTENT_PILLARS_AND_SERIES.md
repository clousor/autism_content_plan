## 2. Deep-Dive Specification for the 10 Core Pillars

---

### Pillar 1: AA | Dr. Atul Explains
*   **Purpose:** Position Dr. Atul Madaan as the definitive, compassionate clinical authority on child development and autism diagnosis in India.
*   **Target Audience:** Segment A (Early Noticers) and Segment C (Post-Diagnosis).
*   **Psychological Principle:** *Authority (Milgram/Cialdini) + Uncertainty Reduction*.
*   **Funnel Stage:** Top of Funnel (Discovery) & Mid Funnel (Trust).
*   **Platforms:** Instagram Reel, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Native Video, YouTube Long-Form anchor.
*   **Ideal Frequency:** 2x per week.
*   **Format & Length:** Talking-head with dynamic multi-angle cuts and on-screen B-roll; 30–45 seconds.
*   **Speaker:** Dr. Atul Madaan, RCI Regd. Clinical Psychologist.
*   **Filming Style:** Eye-level 50mm portrait framing, warm clinical lighting, branded neutral background with Autism Alliance crest.
*   **Hook Patterns:**
    *   *"Speech delay aur autism me ye ek bada farak hai jo 90% parents miss kar dete hain."*
    *   *"Agar aapka bachha naam sun kar nahi dekhta, toh pehle ye test karein."*
    *   *"Autism 'cure' nahi hota, lekin progress bilkul hoti hai. Yahan samajhiye kyun."*
*   **CTA:** *"Apne observation ko note karein aur hamari team se guided checklist lene ke liye 'SIGNS' DM karein."*
*   **Local Adaptation:** Spoken in accessible Hindi/Hinglish; mentions consultations available at Delhi EOK, Faridabad, and Ludhiana.
*   **Paid Promotion Suitability:** High (Tier 1 cold awareness and retargeting).
*   **KPIs:** 3-second hold rate (>65%), Share-to-reach ratio (>3.5%), Saves (>500/post).

### Pillar 2: AA | Parent Asked. Therapist Shows.
*   **Purpose:** Bridge the gap between parental frustration at home and professional clinical technique in the therapy room.
*   **Target Audience:** Segment B (Active Therapy) and Segment A (Early Noticers).
*   **Psychological Principle:** *Cognitive Fluency + Practical Reciprocity*.
*   **Funnel Stage:** Top to Mid Funnel (High Shareability).
*   **Platforms:** Instagram Reel, Facebook Reel, YouTube Shorts.
*   **Ideal Frequency:** 2x per week.
*   **Format & Length:** Therapist demonstration on camera with real therapy materials; 25–35 seconds.
*   **Speaker:** Senior Occupational Therapist / Speech-Language Pathologist / Special Educator.
*   **Filming Style:** Close-up on hands, toys, textured mats, sensory tools; over-the-shoulder demonstration; fast cuts.
*   **Hook Patterns:**
    *   *"Parent ne pucha: 'Mera bachha ek jagah baith kar khana nahi khata.' Hamare OT therapist ne ye dikhaya."*
    *   *"Parent asked: 'How do I stop hand flapping?' Therapist shows: Stop mat kijiye, ye alternative dijiye."*
*   **CTA:** *"Aap apne bachhe ke behavior par kaunsa video chahte hain? Comment karein, hum demonstrate karenge."*
*   **Local Adaptation:** Filmed on location across East of Kailash sensory gym and Crown Plaza Faridabad.
*   **Paid Promotion Suitability:** Exceptional (Organic winner candidate for paid scaling).
*   **KPIs:** Saves-to-reach (>5.0%), DM shares to family members, Follow conversion.

### Pillar 3: AA | 30 Seconds For Parents
*   **Purpose:** Deliver immediate, micro-actionable sensory and communication relief that parents can test within 5 minutes at home.
*   **Target Audience:** All parents (High emotional fatigue, low cognitive bandwidth).
*   **Psychological Principle:** *BJ Fogg Ability Maximization (Low Activation Energy)*.
*   **Funnel Stage:** Discovery & Engagement.
*   **Platforms:** Instagram Reel, YouTube Shorts, Stories.
*   **Ideal Frequency:** 1x per week.
*   **Format & Length:** Rapid single-tip breakdown; 20–30 seconds.
*   **Speaker:** Therapist or Dr. Atul.
*   **Filming Style:** Snappy, high energy, countdown timer on screen (30s $\rightarrow$ 0s).
*   **Hook Patterns:**
    *   *"30 seconds me ek trick: Meltdown ke time bolna band karein, ye 1 cheez karein."*
    *   *"30 seconds home hack: Soap bubbles se eye contact kaise elicit karein."*
*   **CTA:** *"Save this post so you have it ready during your child's next sensory overload."*
*   **Local Adaptation:** Uses standard household items available in any Indian home.
*   **Paid Promotion Suitability:** Moderate.
*   **KPIs:** Saves (>8% of reach), Replays.

### Pillar 4: AA | What Is The Therapist Actually Doing?
*   **Purpose:** De-mystify therapy. Explain the clinical science behind games that look deceptively simple to an untrained eye.
*   **Target Audience:** Segment B (Parents questioning therapy value) and Segment E (Healthcare pros).
*   **Psychological Principle:** *Transparency + Trust Building (Inside the Machine)*.
*   **Funnel Stage:** Mid Funnel (Consideration & Validation).
*   **Platforms:** Instagram Reel, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn Video.
*   **Ideal Frequency:** 1x per week.
*   **Format & Length:** Side-by-side or picture-in-picture commentary; 30–40 seconds.
*   **Speaker:** Lead Occupational Therapist or Speech Therapist.
*   **Filming Style:** Therapist pointing to footage of an exercise (e.g., child rolling on peanut ball) and explaining the vestibular/proprioceptive science.
*   **Hook Patterns:**
    *   *"Aapko lagta hai therapist sirf bacche ke sath ball khel raha hai? Asal me ye 3 hidden skills develop ho rahi hain."*
    *   *"Why does an Occupational Therapist put a child on a swing before teaching table work?"*
*   **CTA:** *"Kya aapke centre me therapy ka logic explain kiya jata hai? Autism Alliance me transparency hamara rule hai."*
*   **Local Adaptation:** Features Autism Alliance therapy rooms explicitly labeled with location watermark.
*   **Paid Promotion Suitability:** High (builds massive differentiation against cheap single-room clinics).
*   **KPIs:** Watch time completion rate, Profile visits.

*   **Filming Style:** Overhead flat-lay of the activity + therapist demonstration.
*   **Hook Patterns:**

*   **CTA:** *"DM 'ACTIVITY' for the printable home play guide."*
*   **Paid Promotion Suitability:** High.
*   **KPIs:** Carousels saves, PDF downloads.

### Pillar 7: AA | Myth vs. Reality
*   **Purpose:** Eradicate pervasive cultural myths in India regarding autism, speech development, diet fads, and developmental delays.
*   **Target Audience:** Broader Indian family ecosystem (Parents, Grandparents, Extended Family).
*   **Psychological Principle:** *Confirmation Bias Disruption + Cognitive Reframing*.
*   **Funnel Stage:** Top of Funnel (Viral Reach).
*   **Platforms:** Instagram Reel, Carousel, Facebook Reel.
*   **Ideal Frequency:** 1x per week.
*   **Format & Length:** Split-screen or pattern-interrupt transition; 25–35 seconds.
*   **Speaker:** Dr. Atul Madaan.
*   **Filming Style:** Direct eye contact, bold red/green graphic overlays ("MYTH" in red / "CLINICAL REALITY" in green).
*   **Hook Patterns:**
    *   *"Myth: 'Bachha bol nahi raha kyunki TV zyada dekhta hai.' Reality: Screen time delay create kar sakta hai, par autism cause nahi karta."*
    *   *"Myth: 'Ladke hamesha der se bolte hain, chinta mat karo.' Reality: Ye advice sabse zyada precious intervention time waste karti hai."*
*   **CTA:** *"Share this with family members who need to hear the scientific truth today."*
*   **Paid Promotion Suitability:** High.
*   **KPIs:** Shares per reach (>4.5%), Family discussion comments.

## 3. The 5 Supporting Pillars

1.  **Supporting Pillar S1: Autism Alliance Team Spotlight:** 30-second humanizing bios of individual therapists (Speech, OT, Special Ed) sharing why they chose this profession. (Builds warmth and familiarity).
2.  **Supporting Pillar S2: School & Inclusion Rights:** Carousels on school accommodations, shadow teachers, and rights under the RTE and RPwD Act 2016. (High save/share among educators).
3.  **Supporting Pillar S3: Live Expert AMA Sessions:** Bi-weekly Live collaborative webinars with guest pediatricians and psychologists.
4.  **Supporting Pillar S4: Parent Process Stories (Ethical & De-identified):** Heartwarming reflections on small victories (e.g., *"First time sitting at the family dinner table for 10 minutes"* without child exposure).
5.  **Supporting Pillar S5: Neurodiversity & Late-Recognized ASD:** Educational breakdowns of masking, sensory overload in older children/teens, and AuDHD (Autism + ADHD).
