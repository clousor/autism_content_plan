## 2. In-Depth Profiles & Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

### Segment A: Early Noticers (Parents of Toddlers Aged 12–36 Months)
*   **Demographic:** Mothers & Fathers aged 26–36; living in urban Delhi NCR, Faridabad, Ludhiana.
*   **Emotional State:** High anxiety, confusion, denial, guilt, hyper-vigilance. Conflicted by family elders telling them *"boys speak late"* or *"it's just screen time"*.
*   **Primary Search Queries:**
    *   *"18 month baby not speaking any words"*
    *   *"does lack of eye contact always mean autism"*
    *   *"baby not responding to name but hears songs"*
    *   *"difference between speech delay and autism"*
*   **Unspoken Fears:** *"Did I cause this by letting my child watch YouTube?"* / *"Will my child ever have a normal life?"*
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"When I notice that my toddler isn't hitting communication or social milestones like other children, help me objectively observe and understand these behaviors at home, so I can seek the right professional evaluation without panicking or blaming myself."*
*   **What Makes Them Follow:** Calm, non-judgmental explanations; clear comparison tables; compassionate tone.
*   **What Makes Them Save & Share:** "Observation Checklists" (e.g., *5 Subtle Signs at 18 Months*); videos they can send to a hesitant spouse or in-laws.
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Free downloadable milestone checklist $\rightarrow$ WhatsApp evaluation inquiry.

---

### Segment B: Parents Already in Therapy (Practical Solution Seekers)
*   **Demographic:** Parents of children aged 3–9 years attending OT, Speech, or Special Ed.
*   **Emotional State:** Exhaustion, therapy fatigue, financial strain, feeling like "nothing is changing fast enough".
*   **Primary Search Queries:**
    *   *"how to handle sensory meltdowns in public"*
    *   *"speech therapy activities to do at home"*
    *   *"stimming games for autistic child"*
    *   *"how many hours of therapy does a child really need"*
*   **Unspoken Fears:** *"Is this therapy actually working, or am I just spending money every month?"*
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"When my child comes home from therapy sessions, give me practical, easy-to-implement home routines and sensory strategies, so I can reinforce their progress in everyday moments without burning out."*
*   **What Makes Them Follow:** Hands-on therapist demonstrations; practical low-cost home activities; honest answers on therapy plateaus.
*   **What Makes Them Save & Share:** 30-second sensory regulation hacks; visual schedules; meltdown vs tantrum decision trees.
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Seeking a second opinion or switching to an integrated multidisciplinary centre (Autism Alliance).

---

### Segment C: Post-Diagnosis Parents (The Overwhelmed Navigators)
*   **Demographic:** Parents whose child received a formal diagnosis (ASD, GDD, ADHD) within the last 0–6 months.
*   **Emotional State:** Grief, sensory overload from medical jargon, paralysis by analysis.
*   **Primary Search Queries:**
    *   *"autism diagnosis mil gaya ab kya karein"*
    *   *"difference between ABA, OT, and speech therapy"*
    *   *"how to get RCI disability certificate in Delhi"*
    *   *"will regular school admit my child with autism"*
*   **Unspoken Fears:** *"Where do I even start? Will my child be rejected by society?"*
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"When we receive a formal developmental diagnosis, give me a clear, step-by-step roadmap of what therapies to prioritize first and how to navigate Indian schools and legal certificates, so our family can move from shock into calm, structured action."*
*   **What Makes Them Follow:** Clear pathway carousels; Dr. Atul's clinical roadmaps; empathetic guidance on the Indian healthcare system.
*   **What Makes Them Save & Share:** Step-by-step guides on RCI certificates, UDID cards, and school rights under the RPwD Act.
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Booking an intake consultation for Autism Alliance’s *Parent Training Program (PTP)* and coordinated therapy plan.

---

### Segment D: Educators & Preschool Teachers
*   **Demographic:** Preschool, nursery, and primary school teachers, special educators, and school counselors across South Delhi, Faridabad, and Ludhiana.
*   **Emotional State:** Overwhelmed by diverse classroom behaviors; wanting to help but fearing confrontation with defensive parents.
*   **Primary Search Queries:**
    *   *"how to manage autistic child in regular classroom"*
    *   *"sensory tools for kindergarten"*
    *   *"how to talk to parents about developmental delay"*
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"When a child in my classroom exhibits sensory distress, non-verbal communication, or social withdrawal, give me compassionate behavioral techniques and the right words to talk to their parents, so we can support the child's inclusion without alienating the family."*
*   **What Makes Them Follow:** Classroom-tested sensory hacks; teacher scripts for parent-teacher meetings; inclusion best practices.
*   **What Makes Them Save & Share:** Graphic guides on *"5 Things Teachers Notice in Class (and How to Share Them Gently)"*.
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Inviting Autism Alliance to conduct teacher training workshops or school screening camps.

---

### Segment E: Pediatricians & Healthcare Professionals
*   **Demographic:** General pediatricians, family physicians, ENT specialists, and pediatric dentists in Delhi NCR and Punjab.
*   **Emotional State:** High patient load, limited consultation time (5–10 mins per child), needing a trustworthy, ethical referral partner.
*   **Primary Search Queries:**
    *   *"RCI registered clinical psychologist child development Delhi"*
    *   *"multidisciplinary pediatric therapy centre Faridabad"*
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"When I screen a child with suspected developmental delay in my OPD, connect me with an accredited, evidence-based multidisciplinary centre led by qualified clinical psychologists, so my patients receive ethical care without quackery or commercial exploitation."*
*   **What Makes Them Follow:** Clinical rigor, case discussions, Dr. Atul’s RCI credentials, scientific citations, transparent methodology.
*   **What Makes Them Save & Share:** Diagnostic screening checklists (M-CHAT guidelines) and interdisciplinary management frameworks.
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Direct professional peer referrals and joint clinical Lives/podcasts.

---

### Segment F: Schools & Inclusive Institutions
*   **Entities:** Prestigious private schools in Delhi NCR (e.g., DPS East of Kailash, Bluebells, Tagore International, Apeejay Faridabad, Sat Paul Mittal Ludhiana).
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"Help our school build a compliant, effective Special Education Needs (SEN) framework, so our institution can provide genuine inclusive education while supporting our faculty and parent community."*
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Institutional partnerships, shadow teacher training, and parent education webinars.

---

### Segment G: Parent Communities & Support Groups
*   **Entities:** Delhi Moms groups, Faridabad parent forums, Ludhiana mothers clubs, WhatsApp special-needs parent circles.
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"Provide our parent community with verified, non-commercial educational resources, so our members feel supported by legitimate experts rather than internet rumors."*
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Collaborative AMA (Ask Me Anything) sessions and community Q&A features.

---

### Segment H: Hyper-Local Delhi NCR & Faridabad Families
*   **Demographic:** Families living within a 15–25 minute drive of East of Kailash (GK, Kalkaji, Nehru Place, Defence Colony) or Crown Plaza Mall Sector 15A Faridabad (Sectors 14, 15, 16, 21, Green Fields, Neharpar).
*   **Primary Pain Point:** Long daily travel in Delhi NCR traffic is disastrous for a sensory-sensitive child. Proximity and ease of parking are critical decision drivers.
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"When choosing a therapy centre, show me that a world-class, clean, welcoming facility exists right in my neighborhood with easy parking and experienced therapists, so therapy doesn't become an exhausting daily battle for my child."*
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Geo-targeted videos showing the exact building entrance, sensory gym, and welcoming staff $\rightarrow$ *"Visit our East of Kailash / Faridabad centre this Saturday"*.

---

### Segment I: Ludhiana & Punjab Families
*   **Demographic:** Families across Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Khanna, Mandi Gobindgarh.
*   **Cultural Context:** Deep family bonds, presence of joint families, high respect for established clinical reputations, preference for Hindi/Punjabi bilingual communication.
*   **JTBD Statement:**
    > *"Provide my family with top-tier developmental therapy right here in Punjab under Dr. Atul Madaan’s direct guidance, so we don't have to constantly travel to Delhi or Chandigarh for quality autism care."*
*   **Conversion Trigger:** Offline consultation booking at Kochar Market Road centre.
