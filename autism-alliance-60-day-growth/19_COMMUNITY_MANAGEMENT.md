# Autism Alliance — Community Management, Comment & DM Triage Protocol

**Document Reference:** Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Public Comments, Direct Messages & Crisis Escalation  
**Compliance Standard:** Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) Ethical Guidelines & Healthcare Communication Safeguards  
**Golden Rule:** **NEVER provide a clinical diagnosis or medical treatment plan in public comments or private social DMs.** Social media is for education, empathy, and triage.

## 2. Safe, Standardized Clinical Response Scripts

### Scenario A: The Remote Diagnosis Request
*   **Parent Query:** *"Mera bachha 2 saal ka hai, wo haath hilata hai aur eye contact nahi deta. Kya use autism hai? Please bataiye main bahut pareshan hoon."*
*   **Safe Response Script:**
    > *"Namaste [Name]. We completely understand aapki chinta, aur ek parent ke taur par aapka dhyan dena bahut swabhavik hai. 
    > 
    > Ek clinical rule hamesha yaad rakhein: Kisi bhi bachhe ko online ya video dekh kar diagnose nahi kiya ja sakta. Haath hilana (stimming) ya eye contact me delay kayi alag developmental ya sensory reasons se ho sakta hai.
    > 
    > Sahi tarika ye hai ki aap kisi RCI-registered Clinical Psychologist se in-person milkar comprehensive developmental observation karwayein.
    > 
    > Agar aap Delhi NCR ya Punjab me hain, toh aap hamare East of Kailash, Faridabad, ya Ludhiana centre par aakar mil sakte hain. Aap hume WhatsApp par message karein (+91 9646 889900) — hamari team aapko guide karegi. Aap akele nahi hain! ✨"*

### Scenario B: The "Can Autism Be Cured?" Query
*   **Parent Query:** *"Doctor sahab, kya autism poori tarah se theek ho sakta hai? Kitne mahine dava chalegi?"*
*   **Safe Response Script:**
    > *"Namaste. Medical science ke anusar Autism koi 'bimari' nahi hai jise dava ya injection se 'cure' kiya jaye; ye ek neurodevelopmental variation hai — yaani bachhe ke brain ka information process karne ka alag tarika.
    > 
    > Lekin iska matlab ye bilkul nahi hai ki bachha seekh nahi sakta! Early Intervention (Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, aur Parent Training) se bachhe communication, self-care, aur social connection me behad shandar progress karte hain aur independent bante hain.
    > 
    > Miracle cure ke daawon se bachein. Sahi evidence-based therapy aur patience hi asli solution hai. Humse paramarsh ke liye link bio me hai."*

### Scenario C: "How Long Will Therapy Take?"
*   **Parent Query:** *"Speech therapy shuru karwayi hai. Kitne mahine me bachha bolne lagega?"*
*   **Safe Response Script:**
    > *"Har bachhe ka neuroplastic pace aur learning curve bilkul unique hota hai. Isliye koi bhi ethical professional '30 din me bolna' guarantee nahi kar sakta.
    > 
    > Progress continuous hoti hai: Pehle joint attention banti hai, phir gestures aur pointing aati hai, phir sounds, aur phir meaningful words.
    > 
    > Jab parents ghar par roz 15–20 minute therapy techniques follow karte hain (Parent-Mediated Model), toh progress 2x se 3x fast ho jati hai. Hamari team se home strategies samajhne ke liye DM karein!"*

### Scenario D: "Which Therapy Does My Child Need?"
*   **Parent Query:** *"Diagnosis me ASD likha hai. Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha main ABA karwaun, OT karwaun ya Speech?"*
*   **Safe Response Script:**
    > *"Ye confusion 90% parents ko hota hai! Ek simple roadmap samajhiye:
    > 1️⃣ Agar bachha restless hai, sensory overload me hai, ya motor skills me delay hai $\rightarrow$ **Occupational Therapy (OT)** pehli priority hoti hai.
    > 2️⃣ Agar eye contact aur baithne ki tolerance hai par words nahi hain $\rightarrow$ **Speech-Language Therapy**.
    > 3️⃣ Agar aggressive behaviors ya routine challenges hain $\rightarrow$ **Behavior Therapy (ABA-based)**.
    > 
    > Autism Alliance me hum in teeno ko alag-alag clinic me bhatakne ke badle **ek hi coordinated plan** ke under provide karte hain. Hamari team se consultation schedule karne ke liye +91 9646 889900 par call karein."*
