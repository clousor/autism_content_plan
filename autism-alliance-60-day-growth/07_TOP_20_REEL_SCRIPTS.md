### SCRIPT 2: Parent Asked. Therapist Shows: The Bubble Trick
*   **Script ID:** `AA-SCR-02`
*   **Presenter:** Senior Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP)
*   **Location:** Therapy Room 2, Autism Alliance Faridabad Centre (Crown Plaza Mall)
*   **Target Duration:** 27 seconds
*   **On-Screen Headline:** "Parent Asked: Eye Contact Kaise Sikhayein?"
*   **Thumbnail Text:** "Don't Force Eye Contact! (Try This Instead)"
*   **Camera Setup:** 45-degree angle, eye-level with child chair, bright colorful therapy backdrop.

#### Timing & Dialogue Breakdown:
*   **0:00 – 0:03 (Hook):**
    *   *Visual:* Therapist sitting on a child-sized mat, holding a colorful bubble tube. She holds the wand right near her own eyes and looks into camera.
    *   *Dialogue (Hinglish):* *"Parent ne pucha: 'Mera bachha eye contact nahi deta, main force kaise karoon?'"*
    *   *Text Overlay:* **NEVER FORCE EYE CONTACT!** (Red highlight box).
*   **0:03 – 0:08 (Scene 1 — The Error):**
    *   *Dialogue (Hindi):* *"Bachhe ka chehra pakad kar eye contact force karna band kijiye. Usse anxiety hoti hai."*
    *   *Visual:* Therapist gently shakes head "No" with empathetic expression.
*   **0:08 – 0:16 (Scene 2 — The Technique Demonstration):**
    *   *Visual:* Therapist dips wand into bubble solution. Blows one bubble, then pauses. She brings the wand directly next to her own eyes and waits in silence.
    *   *Dialogue (Hinglish):* *"Bubbles blow kijiye. Phir pause kijiye. Wand ko apni aankhon ke paas rakhein. Bachha bubble dekhna chahta hai, isliye wo bubble dekhte dekhte aapki aankhon me dekhega."*
*   **0:16 – 0:23 (Scene 3 — The Reinforcement):**
    *   *Visual:* Therapist smiles broadly and immediately blows a burst of bubbles.
    *   *Dialogue (Hinglish):* *"Jaise hi 1 second ka bhi glance mile — immediately blow kijiye aur boliye 'More bubbles!' Natural connection, zero pressure."*
*   **0:23 – 0:27 (CTA):**
    *   *Visual:* Therapist waves to camera with a smile. On-screen text with handle.
    *   *Dialogue (Hinglish):* *"Is trick ko aaj ghar par try karein aur batayein kaisa raha. Aise aur therapy demos ke liye follow karein!"*
    *   *Text Overlay:* **Follow @dratul_autismexpert for Daily Demos**

#### Caption & Metadata:
*   **Caption:**
    > "Bachhe ka sar pakad kar 'meri taraf dekho' kehna band kijiye."
    > 
    > Jab hum kisi neurodivergent bachhe par eye contact force karte hain, toh unka nervous system overwhelmed ho jata hai. Unke liye eye contact uncomfortable ho sakta hai.
    > 
    > Solution? **Joint Attention through Play.**
    > 
    > Soap bubbles ek magical tool hain:
    > 🫧 Wand ko blow karke apni aankhon ke level par layein.
    > 🫧 Pause karein aur bachhe ki curiosity ka wait karein.
    > 🫧 Jaise hi unki nazar wand se hoti hui aapki aankhon par jaye — blow!
    > 
    > Ye simple game bachhe ko sikhata hai ki doosron se connect hona rewarding aur fun hota hai!
    > 
    > 📍 Filmed at Autism Alliance Faridabad (Crown Plaza Mall, Sector 15A).
    > 
    > Share this with a parent who needs fun ways to connect at home! ✨
*   **Compliance Check:** Positive reinforcement; non-forceful; demonstrates neurodiversity-affirming technique.
