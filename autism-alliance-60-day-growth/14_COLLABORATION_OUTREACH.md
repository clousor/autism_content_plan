# Autism Alliance — Collaboration Outreach Operating System

**Document Reference:** Professional Outreach Templates, DMs, Emails & Follow-Up Workflows  
**Standard:** Personalized, respectful, non-transactional, zero spam, clinical dignity.  
**Categories Covered:** Pediatricians/Doctors, Creators/Influencers, Parenting Communities, Schools/Principals, Local Pages, Podcasters, Allied Health Professionals, NGOs.

---

## 1. Outreach Philosophy & Conversion Rules

1.  **Lead With Their Audience’s Unmet Need:** Never start with *"We want to collaborate to grow our followers."* Start with: *"We notice your community frequently asks about delayed milestones, and we would love to provide clear, calm answers from our RCI-registered Clinical Psychologist."*
2.  **Respect Clinical Credentials:** Position Dr. Atul Madaan with quiet authority (RCI CRR No. A56122, MPhil, PhD, Managing Director of Autism Alliance).
3.  **Low Friction:** Make the initial ask effortless (e.g., a 15-minute introductory Zoom call, or a 30-second joint Instagram video concept already scripted).
4.  **Follow-Up Cadence:**
    *   Day 1: Initial DM or Email
    *   Day 4: Polite Value-Add Follow-Up
    *   Day 8: Final Warm Closure (Leaving the door open)
