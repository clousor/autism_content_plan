## 2. City-Specific Paid Campaign Architecture (Recommended Scenario)

### A. DELHI PAID ENGINE (₹1,35,000 / ₹2,250 per Day)
*   **Ad Set D1: South Delhi Cold Reel Winner Scaling (50% Budget — ₹1,125/day):**
    *   *Targeting:* Parents of children 0–5 years within an 8km radius of C-3 East of Kailash (GK-1, GK-2, Kalkaji, EOK, Lajpat Nagar, Defence Colony).
    *   *Creative:* Top-performing organic Reels from `@autismalliance_delhi` that pass the 24-hour green test (Shares/Reach > 3.0%).
    *   *Objective:* Video Engagement / Profile Visits.
*   **Ad Set D2: East of Kailash Centre Familiarity & Tour (20% Budget — ₹450/day):**
    *   *Targeting:* Tight 5km radius around EOK Main Road.
    *   *Creative:* 30-second sensory gym tour + friendly reception welcome.
    *   *Objective:* Local Reach & Map Direction actions.
*   **Ad Set D3: Google High-Intent Search Ads (20% Budget — ₹450/day):**
    *   *Keywords:* `autism centre east of kailash`, `speech therapy south delhi`, `child psychologist near me delhi`.
    *   *Destination:* `autismalliance.in/centres/delhi`.
*   **Ad Set D4: Delhi Parent Circle Event RSVP Ads (10% Budget — Event Weeks):**
    *   *Targeting:* Engaged video viewers & profile visitors from the past 30 days.
    *   *Objective:* WhatsApp event registration confirmations.

### B. FARIDABAD PAID ENGINE (₹1,05,000 / ₹1,750 per Day)
*   **Ad Set F1: Crown Plaza Local Reel Reach (50% Budget — ₹875/day):**
    *   *Targeting:* Parents 0–5 years within a 10km radius of Sector 15A Crown Plaza Mall (Sectors 14–21, Green Fields, Neharpar, NIT).
    *   *Creative:* Top therapist hands-on play demonstration Reels from `@autismalliance_faridabad`.
    *   *Objective:* Instagram Profile Visits & Follower Growth.
*   **Ad Set F2: Mall Parking & Accessibility Tour (25% Budget — ₹437/day):**
    *   *Creative:* Video showcasing basement parking, elevator, and peaceful LG-06 clinic.
    *   *Objective:* Walk-in consultations and direct phone inquiries (+91 9810 578289).
*   **Ad Set F3: Google Local Search Faridabad (25% Budget — ₹438/day):**
    *   *Keywords:* `speech therapist faridabad`, `autism centre sector 15 faridabad`, `child development centre crown plaza`.

### C. LUDHIANA & NATIONAL PAID ENGINE (₹60,000 / ₹1,000 per Day)
*   **Ad Set L1: YouTube In-Feed Search Discovery (50% Budget — ₹500/day):**
    *   *Targeting:* High-intent search queries across North India: `speech delay in hindi`, `autism signs toddlers`, `gestalt language processing`.
    *   *Creative:* Promoting Dr. Atul's 8 Long-Form Masterclasses.
    *   *Objective:* Engaged watch time (>4:30 mins) and YouTube subscribers.
*   **Ad Set L2: Ludhiana Regional Meta & Local Google Search (50% Budget — ₹500/day):**
    *   *Targeting:* 15km radius around Kochar Market Road, Ludhiana.
    *   *Creative:* Dr. Atul clinical videos with Punjabi conversational nuance.
    *   *Objective:* Ludhiana clinic appointments (+91 9646 443200).
