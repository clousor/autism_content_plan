## 3. Production Batching Model: Zero Daily Filming Stress

We do not interrupt daily patient therapy. Filming is restricted to structured monthly windows:
*   **Dr. Atul Master Batch:** 2 Half-Days / Month (3.5 hours) = 16–20 Master Reels + 2 YouTube Masterclasses.
*   **Delhi EOK Centre Shoot:** 2 Mornings / Month (08:30–11:30 AM Pre-OPD) = 12 Local Demonstration Reels.
*   **Faridabad Crown Plaza Shoot:** 2 Midday Blocks / Month (11:30–02:30 PM) = 12 Therapist Reels.
*   **Ludhiana Kochar Market Shoot:** 1 Session / Month = 6 Bilingual Punjab Reels.
*   **Child Dignity Safeguard:** Minor anonymity default (90% hands, swings, and educational tools; zero minor faces published without signed parental consent).
