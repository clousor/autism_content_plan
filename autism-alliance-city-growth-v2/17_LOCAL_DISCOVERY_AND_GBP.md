## 1. Natural Local Search Architecture (Zero Keyword Stuffing)

In local healthcare, search algorithms in 2026 heavily penalize unnatural keyword stuffing (*"best autism centre south delhi speech therapist east of kailash"*). ## 2. Google Business Profile (GBP) 5-Pillar Optimization

For each of the three physical centres, the clinic operations team follows a strict weekly GBP playbook:

### Pillar 1: Precise Categorization & NAP Consistency
*   **Primary Category:** Child Development Centre
*   **Secondary Categories:** Occupational Therapist, Speech Pathologist, Child Psychologist, Special Education School.
*   **Exact Profile Titles:**
    *   *Delhi:* `Autism Alliance — Child Development & Autism Therapy Centre (East of Kailash)`
    *   *Faridabad:* `Autism Alliance — Child Development Centre (Crown Plaza Mall, Sector 15A)`
    *   *Ludhiana:* `Autism Alliance — Child Development & Assessment Centre (Kochar Market Road)`

### Pillar 2: The Weekly Visual Upload Engine (Minimum 5 Photos / Week)
Every Monday, the Social Media Manager uploads 5 fresh high-resolution photos per location:
1.  Exterior facade showing clear signage from main road.
2.  Welcoming reception desk and visitor sign-in lounge.
3.  Sensory gym equipment (swings, balance beams, sensory bins).
4.  Speech therapy acoustic booth with therapy flashcards.
5.  Sanitization in action (therapist wiping down sensory swings).

### Pillar 3: Authentic Review Generation System
*   **SOP:** Front desk coordinators provide visiting parents with a tactile acrylic card featuring a direct QR code:
    > *"Help another anxious parent find hope. Share your experience at Autism Alliance."*
*   **Target:** 3 to 5 new authentic, detailed parent reviews per location every week.
*   **Clinical Guardrail:** Never solicit reviews by promising therapy discounts, and never mention patient diagnostic details publicly.

### Pillar 4: Google Q&A Seeding (Top 5 Local FAQs Answered)
The team seeds and officially answers the most urgent parent search questions directly on the GBP knowledge panel:
1.  *Q:* "Is parking available at Crown Plaza Mall Faridabad?"
    *   *A:* "Yes, ample covered basement parking and direct elevator access to our centre on Lower Ground (LG-06) are available."
2.  *Q:* "Which metro station is closest to Autism Alliance East of Kailash?"
    *   *A:* "We are located on East of Kailash Main Road, directly accessible from Kailash Colony (Violet Line) and Nehru Place (Magenta Line)."
3.  *Q:* "Are all clinical assessments conducted by RCI registered psychologists?"
    *   *A:* "Yes, all diagnostic evaluations and therapy plans are supervised by RCI-registered Clinical Psychologists under CRR No. A56122."

### Pillar 5: Google Business Profile Updates (Google Posts)
*   **Cadence:** 2 Posts per week per centre.
*   **Content:** Summary of the weekly educational Reel + announcement of the upcoming Saturday Parent Classroom.
*   **Call to Action Button:** "Call Now" (`+91 9646 889900`).
