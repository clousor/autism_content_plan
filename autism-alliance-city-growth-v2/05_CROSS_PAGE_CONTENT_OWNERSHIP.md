## 1. The 5 Content Governance Classifications

Every video, carousel, or story asset produced across the network must be assigned one of five explicit distribution protocols before editing:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    THE 5 ASSET CLASSIFICATION RULES                     │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ RULE CLASSIFICATION │ OPERATIONAL MEANING & PLATFORM BOUNDARY           │
├─────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. [ORIGINAL]       │ Published exclusively on the originating page.    │
│                     │ NEVER shared, mirrored, or posted to other feeds. │

│ 2. [ADAPT]          │ Core footage reused, but opening hook (0–3s),     │

│                     │ profile grids via Instagram Collab tool (Max 2/wk)│

```

## 2. Detailed Execution Guidelines by Classification

### A. [ORIGINAL] — City & Brand Exclusives
*   **Definition:** Assets that belong 100% to the local character of that physical location.
*   **Examples:**
    *   *Faridabad:* Walking through Crown Plaza Mall basement parking and taking the elevator to LG-06. (Never post this to Delhi or Dr. Atul).
    *   *Delhi:* Walking through C-3 East of Kailash Main Road with landmark reference to Shri Radha Krishna Mandir.
    *   *Dr. Atul:* Long-form 15-minute clinical podcast interview recordings in a studio.
*   **Enforcement:** Keeps follower feeds distinct and rewards parents who follow both the doctor and their local centre.

### B. [ADAPT] — Master Footage with Localized Framing
*   **Definition:** A high-value clinical explanation or demonstration recorded once, but customized for local audiences through alternate top-of-funnel hooks.
*   **The Adaptation Formula:**
    *   *Step 1 (The Body — 10 to 35 seconds):* Dr. Atul explains: *"When a child covers their ears in noisy spaces, it is sensory hyperacusis, not bad behavior..."*
    *   *Step 2 (The Cut for Dr. Atul — National Hook):* *"Why noise-canceling headphones are not a sign of weakness in autism."*
    *   *Step 3 (The Cut for Delhi — Local Hook):* *"South Delhi parents: What to do when the loud sound of Delhi Metro triggers sensory overload in your toddler."*
    *   *Step 4 (The Cut for Faridabad — Local Hook):* *"Faridabad families: 2 ways to prepare your sensory-sensitive child before entering crowded local markets or Crown Plaza Mall."*
*   **Requirement:** Editors must maintain 3 separate project sequences with distinct voiceover/headline hooks.

### C. [COLLAB POST] — Joint Authority Amplification
*   **Definition:** Utilizing Instagram’s native "Invite Collaborator" feature so a post lives on two profiles simultaneously, pooling comments, likes, and shares.
*   **Frequency Cap:** **Strictly 1 to 2 Collab Posts per week maximum.** (Excessive collabs make the local page look like an empty shadow of Dr. Atul).
*   **Approved Scenarios:**
    1.  *Flagship Milestone:* A major myth-busting Reel featuring Dr. Atul standing inside the East of Kailash sensory gym (`@dratul_autismexpert` $\times$ `@autismalliance_delhi`).
    2.  *Flagship Demonstration:* A senior Faridabad therapist demonstrating speech tools with Dr. Atul introducing the clinical rationale (`@dratul_autismexpert` $\times$ `@autismalliance_faridabad`).
    3.  *Major Offline Event:* Announcing the monthly "Parent Classroom" or local school inclusion seminar.

### D. [CROSS-POST] — Staggered Distribution
*   **Definition:** Sharing evergreen educational carousels or universal demonstrations between pages, but enforcing a **14-day temporal buffer**.
*   **Rule:** If Faridabad publishes a carousel on *"5 Sensory Bin Recipes Using Kitchen Pulses"* on Day 5, the Delhi page CANNOT post it until Day 19.
*   **Rationale:** Prevents shared followers from seeing identical graphics in their feed on the same afternoon.

### E. [DO NOT DUPLICATE] — Strict Clinical & Operational Blacklist
*   **Rule:** The following items are permanently barred from being duplicated across accounts:
    1.  *Direct Contact Details:* Never display Delhi landlines on Faridabad posts, or vice versa.
    2.  *Staff Spotlights:* A therapist working at Crown Plaza Faridabad must never be showcased as staff on the Delhi East of Kailash page.
    3.  *Local Event Registrations:* Event RSVP links must remain exclusive to the hosting city to prevent parent confusion.
    4.  *Raw Identical Video Files:* Uploading the same `.mp4` file without re-hooking is grounds for immediate content rejection.
