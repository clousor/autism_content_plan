## PART 1: THE 5 RECURRING ONLINE LIVE SHOW FORMATS

We reject unstructured, casual Instagram broadcasts. Lives are produced as formatted, predictable **digital television shows**:

1.  **SHOW 1: `Ask Dr. Atul LIVE` (Bi-Weekly / 30–45 Mins):** Dedicated AMA answering pre-submitted parent questions collected from Instagram Story question stickers.
2.  **SHOW 2: `The Parent Circle LIVE` (Monthly / 45 Mins):** Dr. Atul + Co-Host Mother/Advocate discussing maternal mental health, public meltdowns, and emotional acceptance.
3.  **SHOW 3: `One Question. Three Experts.` (Flagship Multidisciplinary / 45 Mins):** Dr. Atul (Psychologist) + Senior OT + Senior Speech Pathologist all answering ONE complex parental dilemma from their distinct clinical lenses (5 minutes each).
4.  **SHOW 4: `School Conversations LIVE` (Bi-Weekly / 40 Mins):** Dr. Atul + Delhi NCR Inclusive School Counselor / Special Educator discussing classroom accommodations, RTE, and RPwD law.
5.  **SHOW 5: `Therapist Lab LIVE` (Bi-Weekly / 30 Mins):** Streamed directly from the sensory gym; Senior Therapists demonstrating physical activities and answering real-time parent technique questions.
