## 1. Editorial Personality & Tone

*   **Tone:** Highly professional, warm, psychologically validating, and academically rigorous.
*   **Aesthetic:** Warm teal, clean architectural lines, serene lighting, and spacious sensory room aesthetics.

### Detailed Pillar Specifications:

1.  **D-P1: Ask The Clinical Expert**
    *   *Purpose:* Address complex parent fears regarding early milestones, neuroplasticity, and autism evaluations.
    *   *Presenter:* Dr. Atul Madaan (RCI Regd. Clinical Psychologist).
    *   *Format:* 45-second vertical Reels + High-contrast cover typography.
    *   *Hook Style:* Pattern interrupt: *"Before you assume your 2-year-old is just stubborn..."*
    *   *CTA:* *"Book a comprehensive evaluation at our East of Kailash centre; link in bio."*
2.  **D-P2: South Delhi Parent Inquiries**
    *   *Purpose:* Real questions gathered from Delhi DMs and clinic waiting rooms.
    *   *Presenter:* Dr. Atul Madaan or Senior Clinical Child Psychologist.
    *   *Format:* 30–45 second direct Q&A.
    *   *Hook Style:* *"A mother in Greater Kailash asked us: 'My child covers their ears in Delhi metro stations...'"*
    *   *CTA:* *"Drop your parenting question in the comments."*
3.  **D-P3: School Inclusion & RPwD Law**
    *   *Purpose:* Empower parents navigating private school nursery admissions, shadow teachers, and CBSE accommodations.
    *   *Presenter:* Special Education Department Lead / Legal Advisor.
    *   *Format:* 6-Slide Saveable Infographic Carousels + Explainer Reels.
    *   *Local Relevance:* Specifically referencing Delhi Directorate of Education (DoE) inclusive mandates.
    *   *CTA:* *"Save this legal rights checklist for your next school PTM."*
4.  **D-P4: Inside East of Kailash (EOK)**
    *   *Purpose:* Environmental transparency to remove fear of visiting the clinic.
    *   *Presenter:* Centre Coordinator & Senior Therapists.
    *   *Format:* Smooth 60fps walking tours of the sensory gym, platform swings, and quiet speech booths.
    *   *Hook Style:* *"South Delhi ke Main Road par ek aisi calm space jahan bachha safe feel karta hai..."*
    *   *CTA:* *"Visit us at C-3 East of Kailash Main Road. Call +91 9646 503200."*
5.  **D-P5: Doctor × Clinical Specialist**
    *   *Purpose:* Peer dialogues between Dr. Atul and South Delhi pediatricians (Apollo, Fortis, Rainbow).
    *   *Presenter:* Dr. Atul + Guest Pediatrician.
    *   *Format:* Split-screen or in-clinic collaborative interview clips.
    *   *CTA:* *"Share this with a parent navigating early milestone checks."*
6.  **D-P6: The Delhi Parent Circle**
    *   *Purpose:* Overcoming isolation and maternal burnout among urban mothers.
    *   *Presenter:* Parenting Educator / Mother Advocate.
    *   *Format:* Relatable storytelling Reels on public meltdowns, sensory outings, and sibling support.
    *   *CTA:* *"Register for our monthly South Delhi Parent Support Circle at EOK."*
7.  **D-P7: Multidisciplinary Milestones**
    *   *Purpose:* Showing how OT, Speech, and Special Ed work in synchronized harmony under one roof.
    *   *Presenter:* Senior OT + Speech Pathologist.
    *   *Format:* 45-second side-by-side demonstration.
    *   *CTA:* *"DM 'ASSESS' to check your child's developmental profile."*
