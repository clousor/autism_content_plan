## 2. City-Specific Filming Run-of-Shows

### Session A: Delhi East of Kailash (C-3 Main Road)
*   **Timing:** 08:30 AM – 11:30 AM (Strictly before full afternoon OPD rush).
*   **Crew On-Site:** Lead Videographer, Growth Strategist, Dr. Atul Madaan, Senior OT, Speech Pathologist.
*   **Minute-by-Minute Run of Show:**
    *   *08:30–09:00:* Equipment rigging, lighting diffusion check, wireless lavalier frequency lock.
    *   *09:00–10:15:* **Dr. Atul Monologue Batch:** Recording 8 clinical hooks (`AA DELHI | Ask The Expert`). Each script executed in 2–3 takes using teleprompter tablet.
    *   *10:15–10:30:* Vocal break & prop transition.
    *   *10:30–11:15:* **Sensory Gym Demonstrations:** Filming Senior OT on platform swing, tactile sensory stepping stones, and crash mats (hands and equipment only).
    *   *11:15–11:30:* Exterior establishing b-roll of C-3 Main Road entrance and reception sign-in desk.

### Session B: Faridabad Crown Plaza Mall (LG-06)
*   **Timing:** 11:30 AM – 02:30 PM (Midday therapist prep window).
*   **Crew On-Site:** Videographer, Faridabad Centre Coordinator, Senior Speech Therapist, Lead OT.
*   **Minute-by-Minute Run of Show:**
    *   *11:30–12:00:* B-roll capture of Crown Plaza Mall basement parking, wide elevator, and walking down into LG-06.
    *   *12:00–01:15:* **Therapist Hands-On Demos:** Filming 6 practical parent routines (`AA FARIDABAD | Therapist Shows`): bubble wand play, spoon-feeding desensitization, rice/rajma sensory bins, PECS picture exchange.
    *   *01:15–02:00:* Centre Q&A: Faridabad clinical supervisor answering 3 real parent dilemmas on camera.
    *   *02:00–02:30:* High-resolution vertical still photos for Instagram Stories, carousels, and Google Business Profile.

### Session C: Ludhiana Regional Heritage Shoot (114 Green Field)
*   **Timing:** 10:00 AM – 02:00 PM (Once per month).
*   **Crew On-Site:** Videographer, Dr. Atul Madaan, Ludhiana Clinical Team.
*   **Minute-by-Minute Run of Show:**
    *   *10:00–11:30:* Dr. Atul recording 6 bilingual Hindi/Punjabi family culture videos (`DR ATUL | Punjab Parent Classroom`).
    *   *11:30–01:00:* Clinical interaction footage: Dr. Atul discussing case progress with Ludhiana therapists (respectful facial interactions; zero patient exposure).
    *   *01:00–02:00:* Local landmark B-roll around Kochar Market Road and Green Field.

## 3. Standard Recording Presets & Hardware Kit

To ensure visual consistency across all three cities without lugging cinema trucks, the videography kit is standardized:

*   **Primary Camera:** iPhone 15 Pro / 16 Pro (ProRes 4K at 30fps for dialogue, 60fps for slow-mo B-roll) or Sony A7C II with 35mm f/1.8 lens.
*   **Audio Rig (Mandatory):** DJI Mic 2 or Rode Wireless PRO dual-channel system. Internal 32-bit float backup recording enabled to prevent clipped audio.
*   **Lighting:** 1x Godox SL-60W LED with 90cm octagonal softbox key light; 1x portable LED wand for subtle rim/accent light.
*   **Gimbal & Tripod:** DJI Osmo Mobile 6 (smooth walking tours) + heavy-duty carbon tripod.
*   **Prompting Tool:** iPad mounted on tripod lens rig using *PromptSmart Pro* (voice-activated speech tracking).
