# Autism Alliance V2 — Event & Live Content Multiplication Engine

**Document Reference:** Operational System for 15x–25x Asset Extraction from Every Major Event, Live & Podcast  
**Production Mandate:** No event exists solely for the room. A 60-minute live gathering is an industrial content studio that feeds the entire multi-city media network for 3–4 weeks.

## 2. The Three-Phase Operational Production Lifecycle

---

### PHASE 1: BEFORE THE EVENT (T-14 Days to T-1 Day)
*   **Objective:** Drive high-intent RSVP registrations and pre-capture digital momentum.
*   **Asset Checklist:**
    1.  *T-14 Days:* Joint Announcement Feed Graphic co-branded with guest/institution.
    2.  *T-10 Days:* 30-Second Video Teaser by Dr. Atul explaining *Why* this event matters.
    3.  *T-7 Days:* Instagram Stories Question Sticker: *"What question do you want answered at the Parent Classroom?"*
    4.  *T-3 Days:* Reel featuring the guest specialist sharing a 15-second teaser.
    5.  *T-24 Hours:* Story Countdown Sticker + Direct WhatsApp RSVP link sticker.

---

#### The Mandatory "5-Minute Guest Content Booth"
Immediately upon conclusion of the event or podcast, while lighting and audio are still active, the guest and Dr. Atul step into the dedicated 9:16 vertical corner for 5 uninterrupted minutes to film **3 high-velocity soundbites**:
*   **Clip 1: The One Myth (45s):**
    *   *Prompt:* *"Doctor/Guest, what is the single biggest myth Indian parents believe about [Topic]?"*
*   **Clip 2: The Urgent Parent Question (45s):**
    *   *Prompt:* *"If an anxious mother notices [Sign] tomorrow morning, what is the very first step she should take?"*
*   **Clip 3: The Professional Takeaway (30s):**
    *   *Prompt:* *"What is the one thing you want every teacher or pediatrician in Delhi NCR to remember?"*

## 3. Strict Child Dignity Safeguard During Events

*   **Audience Seating Rule:** All audience seats face forward toward the speaker.
*   **Camera B Angle:** Positioned strictly behind the last row of parent chairs, capturing over-the-shoulder perspectives to ensure **zero minor or parent faces are captured without explicit written authorization**.
*   **Microphone Etiquette:** When parents ask questions during Q&A, the roaming mic captures audio while the camera remains focused on Dr. Atul answering with empathy.
