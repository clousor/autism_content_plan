# Autism Alliance V2 — Complete Run-of-Shows for 6 Offline Regional Events

**Document Reference:** Operational Playbooks, Run-of-Shows, Logistics & Content Capture Guides for Offline Parent Gatherings  
**Planning Window:** 60 Days (September – November 2026)  
**Venues:** In-Centre Sensory Hubs at Delhi East of Kailash, Faridabad Crown Plaza Mall, and Ludhiana Kochar Market Road  
**Core Policy:** Free RSVP, Intimate Attendance (30–40 Parents Max), Minor Anonymity Default, Zero Heavy-Handed Sales.

## SIGNATURE FRANCHISE BLUEPRINT: `The Autism Alliance Parent Classroom`

*   **Positioning:** India's premier community-first neurodevelopmental coaching masterclass for parents.
*   **Visual Identity:** Warm Slate & Gold banner with clean typography: *"Empowering Parents as the Real Therapists"*.
*   **Monthly Rotation:** Alternates bi-weekly between Delhi East of Kailash, Faridabad Crown Plaza Mall, and Ludhiana.
*   **Registration Funnel:** Simple mobile-optimized Google Form or WhatsApp click-to-register (Name, City, Child's Age, Primary Concern). Capped strictly at 35 seats to preserve intimacy and question time.
*   **Follow-Up Community Loop:** Attendees are inducted into an exclusive, moderated WhatsApp Broadcast Group receiving weekly therapist activity PDFs and priority appointment slots.
