### Topic 1: What Parents Misunderstand in the First 6 Months After an Autism Diagnosis
*   **Hook:** *"The biggest trauma isn't the diagnosis itself; it's the 6 months of chaotic panic that follow. Here is what parents should actually do first."*
*   **Ideal Host Fit:** Mansi Zaveri (*Kidsstoppress*) or Maitri Verma (*Little Fixes*).
*   **Target Audience:** Parents who have received a developmental assessment within the last 180 days.
*   **Key Talking Points:**
    1.  Why rushing to 4 different clinics for 25 hours a week causes catastrophic child and maternal burnout.
    2.  The difference between "fixing deficits" and establishing emotional co-regulation.
    3.  How to organize medical documentation (RCI psychologist report, hearing audiology, UDID card).
    4.  Why home routines matter 10x more than clinic session hours.
*   **Viral Clip Opportunities (3 Clips):**
    *   *Clip 1A:* Stop putting your 2-year-old in 30 hours of clinic therapy.
    *   *Clip 1B:* The 3 documents you actually need after an autism diagnosis.
    *   *Clip 1C:* Grieving the imaginary child vs loving the real child in front of you.
*   **CTA:** *"Download our Free Post-Diagnosis 30-Day Parent Action Roadmap at autismalliance.in."*
*   **Clinical Risk Mitigation:** Must clarify that early intervention is crucial, but quality of parent interaction supersedes exhausting tabletop drill hours.

### Topic 2: Speech Delay vs. Developmental Differences: When Is Waiting Dangerous?
*   **Hook:** *"Indian elders always say 'Ladke der se bolte hain'. Why this single sentence costs children their most precious window of neuroplasticity."*
*   **Ideal Host Fit:** Gunjan Taneja (*Gunjan Talks*) or Dr. Arpit Gupta.
*   **Target Audience:** Parents of toddlers aged 15 to 36 months noticing silent milestones.
*   **Key Talking Points:**
    1.  What is Joint Attention and why pointing with an index finger is more important than spoken words.
    2.  Auditory processing vs hearing loss: Why the BERA test must be completed first.
    3.  The biological reality of the 0–3 age window: Rapid synaptic pruning and neuroplasticity.
*   **Viral Clip Opportunities:**
    *   *Clip 2A:* The pointing milestone: Does your child show you what they see?
    *   *Clip 2B:* Why waiting until age 4 to start therapy is a medical tragedy.
    *   *Clip 2C:* Receptive language vs expressive speech: Understanding without speaking.
*   **CTA:** *"Book a clinical milestone observation at Autism Alliance centres in Delhi, Faridabad, or Ludhiana."*
*   **Clinical Risk Mitigation:** Emphasize that speech delay alone does not equal autism; comprehensive multidisciplinary evaluation is required.

### Topic 4: Screen Time, "Virtual Autism" and Unearned Parental Guilt
*   **Hook:** *"Did giving your child an iPad cause their autism? Let's separate medical science from toxic maternal guilt."*
*   **Ideal Host Fit:** Dr. Vibha Krishnamurthy (*Parenting Aaj Kal*) or Raj Shamani (*Figuring Out*).
*   **Target Audience:** Modern urban mothers suffering acute guilt over early screen exposure.
*   **Key Talking Points:**
    1.  Autism is a complex neurodevelopmental variation with genetic foundations; screens do NOT cause ASD.
    2.  What "Virtual Autism" / Screen-Induced Delay actually is: Lack of reciprocal 3D human interaction.
    3.  The Screen Detox protocol: How to replace passive screen time with high-sensory real-world play without panic.
*   **Viral Clip Opportunities:**
    *   *Clip 4A:* Can screens cause autism? The definitive medical answer.
    *   *Clip 4B:* Stop telling mothers they ruined their children with phones.
    *   *Clip 4C:* The 1-hour replacement rule for toddler screen addiction.
*   **CTA:** *"DM 'SCREENS' for our free Screen-Free Sensory Play Alternative Guide."*
*   **Clinical Risk Mitigation:** Acknowledge negative impacts of excessive screens on developing brains without using fear-mongering terms.

### Topic 7: The Sensory World: Why Your Child Crashes into Sofas and Screams at Haircuts
*   **Hook:** *"If you think humans have only 5 senses, you will never understand an autistic child. Meet the Vestibular and Proprioceptive systems."*
*   **Ideal Host Fit:** Dr. Sid Warrier (*Neuroscience Podcast*).
*   **Target Audience:** Parents perplexed by hyperactivity, toe walking, spinning, and extreme sound sensitivity.
*   **Key Talking Points:**

    2.  Why a haircut feels like physical torture to a child with tactile defensiveness.
    3.  The science of "heavy work": Why jumping, pushing walls, and deep pressure organize a disorganized brain.
*   **Viral Clip Opportunities:**

### Topic 8: Echolalia and Gestalt Language: When Repeating Commercials Is Actually Speech
*   **Hook:** *"Your child repeats entire YouTube dialogues word-for-word, but won't answer 'What is your name?'. They are not broken; they are a Gestalt Language Processor."*
*   **Ideal Host Fit:** *Kidsstoppress* or *Little Fixes*.
*   **Target Audience:** Parents of echolalic toddlers and pre-schoolers.

    3.  How to use declarative language (*"Hum bahar ja rahe hain"*) to unlock meaningful communication.
*   **Viral Clip Opportunities:**
    *   *Clip 8A:* Stop quizzing your toddler: The question trap.
    *   *Clip 8B:* Why reciting Peppa Pig episodes is real language progress.
    *   *Clip 8C:* Gestalt Language Processing decoded in 60 seconds.
