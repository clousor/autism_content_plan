## 1. Verification Standards & Methodology

To ensure absolute operational credibility, Autism Alliance enforces strict evidence criteria:
*   **[VERIFIED]:** Direct public profile link confirmed active in last 30 days, official email or telephone verified via official website/registry, physical address confirmed on Google Maps/MCA registry.
*   **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]:** Entity confirmed to exist with physical presence or active page, but specific personal contact route (direct email) requires front-desk confirmation during initial outreach.
*   **[UNVERIFIED]:** Community suggestion or directory mention with insufficient recent public activity; requires manual vetting before outreach.
