## 2. Platform Mandates by City

### A. DELHI ECOSYSTEM
*   **Channels:** Dedicated Instagram Page (`@autismalliance_delhi`) + Dedicated Facebook Page (`Autism Alliance Delhi`).
*   **Strategic Role:** The **"South Delhi Clinical & Educational Hub"**.
*   **Primary Geography:** East of Kailash (C-3 Main Road), Greater Kailash (GK-1 & GK-2), Kailash Colony, Kalkaji, CR Park, Lajpat Nagar, Nehru Place, New Friends Colony, Defence Colony, South Extension.
*   **Content Focus:** Professional authority, comprehensive developmental evaluations, inclusive private school collaborations, South Delhi parent support circles, and high-end sensory gym walkthroughs.
*   **Tone:** Prestigious, academically grounded, empathetic, and tailored to educated urban nuclear families.

### B. FARIDABAD ECOSYSTEM
*   **Channels:** Dedicated Instagram Page (`@autismalliance_faridabad`).
*   **Facebook Recommendation (Evidence-Based Decision):**
    > **DECISION: OPTION B (Master Facebook Page + Hyper-Local Geo Ads).**  
    > *Rationale:* A dedicated Faridabad Facebook page will suffer severe algorithmic penalties, low organic reach, and duplicate administrative burden. In Faridabad, Facebook users are heavily consolidated in community groups (e.g., *Voice of Faridabad*, *Faridabad Moms Group*). We will distribute Faridabad video assets through the verified master Facebook page (`Dr. Atul Madaan - Autism Expert`) using Meta Ads Manager geo-fencing (10km around Crown Plaza Mall Sector 15A). This ensures high-trust delivery without audience dilution.
*   **Strategic Role:** The **"Hands-On Therapy & Community Demonstration Hub"**.
*   **Primary Geography:** Sector 15A (Crown Plaza Mall), Sector 14, 15, 16, 17, 21, NIT Faridabad, Old Faridabad, Greater Faridabad (Neharpar), Green Fields Colony, Charmwood.
*   **Content Focus:** Real Occupational Therapy, Speech-Language Pathologists in action, tactile sensory activities, daily centre routines, Crown Plaza Mall accessibility (effortless parking/elevators), and grassroots parent meetups.
*   **Tone:** Highly practical, warm, community-driven, and accessible to multi-generational residential families.

### C. LUDHIANA / DR. ATUL MADAAN ECOSYSTEM
*   **Channels:** Primary Instagram (`@dratul_autismexpert`), Primary Facebook (`dratulmadaanautismexpert`), and Primary YouTube (`@dratulmadaan_autismexpert`).
*   **Strategic Role:** Dual-Function Engine:
    *   *Role 1 (National):* High-authority clinical myth-busting, national podcast appearances, search-led YouTube masterclasses, and medical policy education.
    *   *Role 2 (Punjab Regional):* Local anchor for Ludhiana families (114 Green Field, Kochar Market Road), regional hospital referrals (DMCH/CMCH), and bilingual Punjabi/Hindi guidance for joint families.
*   **Content Balance:** 70% National Evergreen Authority / 30% Ludhiana & Punjab Regional Community Presence.

## 3. Cross-Page Collaboration, Tagging & Anti-Cannibalization Rules

Publishing the same content identically across three pages destroys algorithmic distribution and bores shared followers. We enforce strict interaction protocols:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    CROSS-PAGE INTERACTION PROTOCOLS                     │
├────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ FEATURE / ACTION   │ STRICT GOVERNANCE RULE                             │
├────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Collab Post     │ Reserved for FLAGSHIP MOMENTS ONLY (1x to 2x per   │
│    (Joint Feed)    │ week max). E.g., Dr. Atul master explainer filmed  │
│                    │ inside East of Kailash can be co-authored between  │
│                    │ @dratul_autismexpert and @autismalliance_delhi.    │
├────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Cross-Tagging   │ Regular daily Reels do NOT use the Collab tool.    │
│    (@mentions)     │ Local pages tag Dr. Atul in caption/audio:         │
│                    │ "Guided by @dratul_autismexpert at our EOK centre."│
├────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Repurposing &   │ NEVER upload raw identical files simultaneously.   │
│    Adaptations     │ A national video must be re-cut with a local hook: │
│                    │ "South Delhi parents ask..." before posting locally│
├────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Never Duplicated│ Hyper-local staff introductions, specific centre   │
│    (Exclusive)     │ open hours, local event registrations, and direct  │
│                    │ phone desk BTS remain 100% EXCLUSIVE to that city. │
└────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 5. Preventing Profile Cannibalization: The 3 Visually Distinct Grid Layouts

When a parent visits all three profiles, they must immediately see three distinctly branded experiences:

1.  **Dr. Atul Madaan Profile Grid:**
    *   *Visual Identity:* Deep Navy Blue & White medical authority palette.
    *   *Grid Feel:* The "Clinical Professor's Digital Library". Professional portraits, bold question headlines, podcast clips, and high-level medical explanations.
2.  **Delhi East of Kailash Profile Grid:**
    *   *Visual Identity:* Warm Teal, Slate & Gold urban sophistication palette.
    *   *Grid Feel:* The "South Delhi Modern Child Development Hub". Highlighting developmental milestones, school inclusion carousels, child psychology, and serene sensory room walkthroughs.
3.  **Faridabad Crown Plaza Profile Grid:**
    *   *Visual Identity:* Energetic Soft Coral, Mint & Warm Wood palette.
    *   *Grid Feel:* The "Active Sensory & Therapy Playground". Macro hands-only clips, colorful therapy tools, step-by-step parent home routines, and cheerful therapist demonstrations.
