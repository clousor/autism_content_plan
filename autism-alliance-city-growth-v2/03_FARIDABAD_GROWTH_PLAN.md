## 1. Editorial Personality & Tone

Faridabad represents a dynamic mix of established industrial business families, corporate professionals commuting to South Delhi, and multi-generational joint households. Parents here are pragmatic: they want to know **what actually happens inside the therapy room**, how to manage sensory behaviors at home, and how easy it is to visit the centre.
*   **Tone:** Down-to-earth, energetic, deeply practical, and action-oriented.
*   **Aesthetic:** Warm wood, colorful tactile equipment, clean therapy tables, smiling therapist faces, and macro close-ups of activities.
*   **Language Mix:** 85% conversational Hindi/Hinglish / 15% simple English terms.
*   **Key Friction Removed:** **Parking and Accessibility.** Emphasize Crown Plaza Mall's basement parking, elevator access, and peaceful lower-ground environment.
