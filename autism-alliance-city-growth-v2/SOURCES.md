## 5. Medical, Clinical & Advertising Compliance Standards (2026)

*   **RCI Code of Ethics (1992):** Strict prohibition of misleading claims, unscientific cure promises, and non-evidence-based therapies.
*   **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP 2023):** Mandates explicit verifiable parental consent before processing or publishing any data or media involving children.
*   **Meta Healthcare Ads Policy (2026):** Prohibits targeting personal sensitive attributes (health conditions/autism). Targeting relies on geo-fencing (5–10km radius) and parent demographic stacking (Parents of 0–5 year olds).
*   **National Tele-Mental Health Programme (Tele-MANAS):** Emergency crisis triage number 14416 cited in clinical protocols.
