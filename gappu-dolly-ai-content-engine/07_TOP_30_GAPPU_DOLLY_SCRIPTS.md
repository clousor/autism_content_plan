## REEL 01: Why Don't You Look At Me? (`GD-R001`)

*   **Pillar:** P1: GAPPU ASKS
*   **Setting:** Living Room
*   **Characters:** Gappu & Dolly (Supporting: Kabir (Child))
*   **Core Concept:** Explaining why forced eye contact impairs auditory processing in autistic children.
*   **Clinical Review Status:** `CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED`
*   **Primary Hook:** *"Dolly, why does our friend look at the wall when we talk?"*

### SCENE 1 (0:00 – 0:03) — THE HOOK & SENSORY TRIGGER
*   **Background Environment:** Living Room rendered in soft 3D stylized lighting.
*   **Camera Position:** Eye-level Medium-Close Up (MCU), fast dynamic push-in on 35mm lens.
*   **Visual & Action:** Gappu sitting, observing the unfolding tension. On-screen tension arises.
*   **Character Expression:** Gappu's eyes wide in gentle wonder, ears flaring slightly, trunk tip curling up in curious questioning.
*   **Dialogue (Gappu):** *"Dolly, why does our friend look at the wall when we talk?"*
*   **On-Screen Text (Hook Banner):** **DOLLY, WHY DOES OUR FRIEND LOOK AT THE WALL W...** (Bright yellow font with navy border).
*   **Sound & Music:** Ambient gentle chime followed by an inquisitive harp plucking note.
*   **Transition:** Swift whip-pan to 3/4 wide angle.

### SCENE 2 (0:03 – 0:10) — THE MISUNDERSTANDING DECONSTRUCTED
*   **Background Environment:** Living Room interior with warm pastel depth-of-field.
*   **Camera Position:** Wide Medium Shot showing Gappu and the unfolding situation.
*   **Visual & Action:** Dolly appears gracefully, swimming through weightless sensory space with sparkling water droplets trailing her tail.
*   **Character Expression:** Dolly smiling with deep empathy, head tilted, eyes radiating warmth and reassurance.
*   **Dialogue (Dolly):** *"Gappu, when expression looks different, it doesn't mean anything is missing! Look closer."*
*   **On-Screen Text:** *"DIFFERENT EXPRESSION ≠ LACK OF UNDERSTANDING"*
*   **Sound & Music:** Gentle water splash shimmer sound effect; soft, warm acoustic guitar chords entering.
*   **Transition:** Smooth match-cut into macro perspective.

### SCENE 3 (0:10 – 0:20) — THE NEURODEVELOPMENTAL INSIGHT
*   **Background Environment:** Stylized warm visualization / subtle glowing aura showing sensory brain dynamics.
*   **Camera Position:** Dynamic over-the-shoulder tracking shot circling Dolly as she explains.
*   **Visual & Action:** Dolly uses her pectoral flipper to illustrate looking away reduces cognitive overload and helps the child listen better.. Gappu listens intently, touching his trunk to his cheek in understanding.
*   **Character Expression:** Gappu nodding in deep realization (*'Aha!'* expression), eyes sparkling with clarity.
*   **Dialogue (Dolly):** *"Looking away reduces cognitive overload and helps the child listen better."*
*   **Dialogue (Gappu):** *"So it's not bad behavior... it's a different way to connect!"*
*   **On-Screen Text (Key Takeaway):** **LOOKING AWAY REDUCES COGNITIVE OVERLOAD ** (Clear white subtitles in safe zone).
*   **Sound & Music:** Uplifting xylophone sequence building emotional warmth.
*   **Transition:** Soft dissolve to practical resolution.

### SCENE 4 (0:20 – 0:26) — THE EMPATHETIC ADJUSTMENT & RESOLUTION
*   **Background Environment:** Living Room transformed into a calm, supportive, harmonious space.
*   **Camera Position:** Warm Low-Angle Two-Shot framing Gappu and Dolly side-by-side with child.
