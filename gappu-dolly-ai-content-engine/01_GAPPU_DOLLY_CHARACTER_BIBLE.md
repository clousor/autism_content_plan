## PART 1: THE FOUNDATIONAL PHILOSOPHY

Gappu and Dolly were conceived by Autism Alliance not as decorative mascots, but as **empathy translators** for Indian society. In neurodevelopmental care, children on the spectrum are frequently misjudged: when a child does not make direct eye contact, does not respond verbally to a shouted name, or flaps their hands in joy, adults erroneously assume a lack of intelligence or an inability to feel emotion.

Gappu and Dolly directly dismantle this tragic misconception:
*   **GAPPU (The Elephant):** Embodies **Intelligence, Memory & Deep Internal Understanding**. He proves that understanding exists powerfully beneath the surface, even when external expression is non-traditional.
*   **DOLLY (The Dolphin):** Embodies **Emotional Sensitivity, Social Warmth & Empathy**. She proves that the desire to connect and feel is profound, even when social signals look different.

Together, they represent the complete, unbroken humanity of every neurodivergent child.

```
        ▼                                                             ▼
[FACIAL ARCHITECTURE]                                         [BODY SILHOUETTE]
• Head-to-Body Ratio: 1 : 1.2 (Chibi/Storybook)               • Plump, rounded pear-shaped torso

• Trunk: Gentle upward S-curve; 3 delicate bridge wrinkles     • Seated height: 85cm (friendly toddler eye-level)

```

### 1. Visual Taxonomy & Proportions
*   **Species:** Stylized Asian Elephant (*Elephas maximus*), baby/juvenile proportions.
*   **Apparent Age:** 5 to 7 years old in emotional curiosity, but possesses timeless, unhurried wisdom.
*   **Head-to-Body Ratio:** `1 : 1.2`. The head is prominent, circular, and friendly, signaling cognitive depth and approachability.
*   **Height & Stature:** When standing: 95cm tall; when seated: 70cm tall. Designed to sit naturally on the floor alongside playing children and parents.

### 2. Facial Features & Anatomy
*   **Eyes:** Large, dark obsidian button eyes (`#1A202C`) positioned wide on the face. Each eye features **two crisp specular highlights**: a primary 10 o'clock circular highlight (`#FFFFFF`) and a smaller 4 o'clock micro-catchlight.
*   **Eyebrows:** Subtle, mobile curved expressive brows that rise in curiosity and tilt gently in empathy. Never sharp or angry.
*   **Ears:** Expansive, fan-shaped ears with undulating soft edges. The outer rim curves gently backward; the interior ear fold features a soft gradient of periwinkle blue (`#BEE3F8` to `#7AB8E8`). Ears flutter gently when Gappu is processing a new idea or feeling comfortable.
*   **Trunk:** Short to medium length, graceful upward S-curve. The bridge of the trunk has 3 soft horizontal skin creases. The tip is rounded and prehensile, capable of holding a crayon, pointing to a PECS card, or comforting Dolly.
*   **Mouth & Smile:** Sweet, gentle upward curved smile line tucked beneath the trunk base. Never wide-gaping or exaggerated.
*   **Cheeks:** Prominent, chubby rounded cheeks adorned with soft baby-pink watercolor blush (`#FFB6C1`, 30% opacity).

### 3. Body Silhouette & Physical Form
*   **Torso:** Pear-shaped, softly rounded belly that rests comfortably on the ground when seated. Zero sharp corners; every contour is organic and plush.
*   **Limbs:** Sturdy, columnar legs with soft, circular feet. Each foot features four rounded, pale periwinkle toenail pads (`#D0E8F9`). Arms are soft, rounded, and capable of expressive, gentle gestures (touching his chin in thought, open-palm offering).
*   **Tail:** Short, playful elephant tail with a tiny soft tuft at the tip, wagging gently when content.

### 4. Color Palette & Surface Texture
*   **Base Skin:** Soft Sky / Ice Blue (`#BEE3F8` to `#EAF4FC`).
*   **Shadow Contours:** Gentle Periwinkle Blue (`#7AB8E8`).
*   **Cheek Blush:** Baby Pink (`#FFB6C1`).
*   **Texture:** Subtle matte velvet clay with soft watercolor shading. Subsurface scattering allows warm light to illuminate ear edges.

### 5. Clothing & Attire Protocols
*   **Classic Storybook Form (Default):** Natural, unclad form with soft skin gradients, as seen in the official Autism Alliance master reference artwork.
*   **Centre & Community Form:** Branded Autism Alliance Polo Shirt:
    *   *Color:* Deep Royal Blue (`#185EA9`) with clean white collar piping.
    *   *Emblem:* Official Autism Alliance stylized child-silhouette logo embroidered on the left chest.
    *   *Fit:* Comfortable, tailored around his rounded tummy with custom sleeve openings.

### 6. Personality & Character Archetype
*   **The Philosophical Observer:** Thoughtful, patient, highly observant, and deeply curious.
*   **Cognitive Style:** Logical, pattern-seeking, loves routines, fascinated by how things work. He processes information thoroughly before speaking.
*   **Vocal Energy:** Measured, gentle, reassuring, and slightly questioning.
*   **Humor:** Gentle situational wonder. He never mocks, never clowns, and never behaves foolishly.
*   **Signature Behavior:** Gently touching his trunk to his cheek when contemplating a complex question (*"Hmm, Dolly... what if..."*).

## PART 3: DOLLY — THE DOLPHIN (DESIGN BIBLE)

```
                       ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                       │    DOLLY: DESIGN SCHEMATIC    │
                       └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                       │
        ┌──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                                             ▼
[AERODYNAMIC ANATOMY]                                         [EMOTIONAL AURA]
• Species: Stylized Bottlenose Dolphin (Warm & Sleek)         • Leaps with weightless fluidity
• Dorsal Color: Deep Azure/Cobalt Blue (#2B95E9)              • Creates soft sensory water ripples
• Ventral Color: Crisp Pure Alice White (#F0F8FF)             • Cheek: Warm Peach/Coral Blush (#FFAAA6)
• Rostrum: Gentle upward beak with permanent warm smile       • Eye: Radiant glossy black with joyful sparkle
```

### 1. Visual Taxonomy & Proportions
*   **Species:** Stylized Bottlenose Dolphin (*Tursiops truncatus*), youthful and aerodynamic.
*   **Apparent Age:** 6 to 8 years old in emotional maturity; acts as an intuitive guide and emotional compass.
*   **Body Line:** Elegant, curved C-arc silhouette. Dolly is almost always depicted in dynamic, graceful motion—arching over Gappu, diving playfully, or hovering gently.
*   **Length:** 105cm from beak tip to tail fluke.

### 2. Facial Features & Anatomy
*   **Rostrum (Beak):** Smooth, rounded beak with a gentle upward contour that gives her a permanent, welcoming, compassionate expression.
*   **Melon (Forehead):** Smooth, rounded, radiant forehead that glows with subtle luminescence when she explains emotional feelings.
*   **Eyes:** Sparkling, dark sapphire-black eyes (`#0D1E36`) with a large star-like specular highlight and curved lower lid that squints into pure joy when smiling.
*   **Cheeks:** Warm peach-coral blush (`#FFAAA6`) highlighting her empathetic facial expressions.

### 3. Body Silhouette & Physical Form
*   **Dorsal Fin:** Prominent, curved, shark-free dorsal fin on her upper back with smooth rounded tip.
*   **Pectoral Flippers:** Two expressive, wing-like pectoral fins. Dolly uses these flippers like expressive hands—clapping softly, gesturing toward someone in distress, or resting a flipper on Gappu's shoulder.
*   **Caudal Fluke (Tail):** Wide, symmetrical tail fluke with a delicate median notch. In motion, her tail leaves a trail of sparkling water droplets and rainbow luminescence.

### 4. Color Palette & Surface Texture
*   **Dorsal (Back & Sides):** Rich, vibrant Azure Blue (`#2B95E9`) graduating into deep ocean cobalt (`#1662B5`) along the spine.
*   **Ventral (Belly & Throat):** Pure, clean Alice White (`#F0F8FF`) with a satin sheen.
*   **Specularity:** Smooth, lustrous satin finish reflecting soft studio key lights, conveying cleanliness, hydration, and emotional clarity.

### 5. Movement & Terrestrial Mechanics
Because Dolly is an aquatic mammal appearing in terrestrial settings (classrooms, living rooms, therapy sensory gyms), she moves via **Magical Sensory Buoyancy**:
*   She moves through the air as if swimming in a gentle, warm, invisible ocean of sensory calm.
*   She is often accompanied by soft, translucent water droplets, gentle ripples, or a floating sensory ribbon.
*   In stationary moments (listening in class or clinic), she rests comfortably on a soft blue beanbag, sensory peanut ball, or leans affectionately against Gappu.

### 6. Personality & Character Archetype
*   **The Empathetic Bridge:** Emotionally perceptive, socially intuitive, warm, and deeply compassionate.
*   **Cognitive Style:** Senses emotional atmospheres immediately. When a child is overwhelmed, Dolly feels the sensory intensity before anyone else notices.
*   **Vocal Energy:** Melodic, encouraging, warm, articulate, and soothing.
*   **Humor:** Joyful, playful wordplay and warm encouragement.
*   **Signature Behavior:** Arching gracefully in a loop and resting her chin gently beside Gappu's ear to whisper an emotional insight.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              "THE HAPPY FRIENDS OF AUTISM ALLIANCE"                     │

├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│                            │  the eyes don't have to work so hard!"     │

```

*   **Official Status:** Best companions and mutual guides ("Happy Friends of Autism Alliance").
*   **Strict Prohibitions:**
    *   They are **NOT** siblings (avoids family dynamics that distract from universal friendship).
    *   They are **NOT** romantic partners (strictly non-romantic, innocent, peer companions).
    *   They are **NOT** parent and child (both possess child-like wonder with adult clinical clarity).
*   **The Cognitive-Emotional Flywheel:**
    *   Gappu provides **Structure, Logic, Memory & Careful Observation**.
    *   Dolly provides **Warmth, Sensory Awareness, Emotional Validation & Intuition**.
    *   Neither is ever portrayed as superior to the other; true understanding requires both the elephant's mind and the dolphin's heart.
