## 2. The First 3 Priority Pilots (To Animate First)

Before generating videos 4 through 10, the team executes and tests **Pilot 1, Pilot 2, and Pilot 3**:

---

### PILOT 1: "They Understand More Than We Think" (Flagship Emotional Anchor)
*   **Format:** 35-Second Micro-Story (Format 1).
*   **Characters:** Gappu & Dolly (Mascots only).
*   **Setting:** Clean, glowing reflective studio with soft pastel watercolor floor.
*   **The Creative Hypothesis:** Emotional validation without medical lecturing will achieve a **Share-to-Reach ratio > 4.5%**, establishing Gappu & Dolly as trusted family icons.
*   **Exact Hook:** *"When expression looks different, it's easy to assume something is missing. But nothing is missing."*
*   **Narrative Arc:**
    *   *0:00–0:08:* Gappu sits with head down, holding a single puzzle piece. An echoing voice asks: *"Why doesn't he answer?"*
    *   *0:08–0:20:* Dolly leaps gracefully overhead, trailing sparkling water ripples. She gently whispers to the audience: *"He understands your words. He feels your love. He connects... just in his own quiet way."*
    *   *0:20–0:28:* Gappu smiles, placing the puzzle piece perfectly into a heart-shaped board. Both mascots look into camera with glowing warmth.
    *   *0:28–0:35:* Outro: *"Autism Alliance — Parents First. Always."*
*   **Primary Platform:** Instagram Reels & Facebook Reels (Boosted with ₹1,000 cold testing budget).
*   **Success Benchmark:** 500+ Saves, 350+ Shares, >65% 3-second hook retention.

---

### PILOT 2: "The Pressure Cooker Panic" (Relatable Family Humor)
*   **Format:** 28-Second Wrong Way vs Better Way (Format 3).
*   **Characters:** Gappu, Dolly, Maa, Stylized Kabir.
*   **Setting:** Modern Indian Modular Kitchen.
*   **The Creative Hypothesis:** Situational Indian kitchen tension will attract family elders and fathers who normally ignore medical advice.
*   **Exact Hook:** *"Dolly, why did Kabir run away when the pressure cooker whistled?"*
*   **Narrative Arc:**
    *   *0:00–0:05:* Pressure cooker blasts loud steam: *Pshhhhhh!* Kabir covers ears and ducks under dining table.
    *   *0:05–0:14:* Gappu looks bewildered. Dolly visualizes the sound wave as a giant buzzing horn inside Kabir's ears: *"To sensitive ears, a sudden whistle feels like a thunderclap right next to your pillow!"*
    *   *0:14–0:22:* Better Way: Gappu gives Kabir a 10-second warning before whistle (*"Kabir, cooker whistle coming!"*) and hands him cute periwinkle ear defenders. Kabir gives a thumbs up!
    *   *0:22–0:28:* Outro & CTA: *"Save this hack for your kitchen!"*
*   **Success Benchmark:** >4.0% Save rate, high comments tagging spouses (*"Dekho ye hamare ghar me hota hai"*).

---

### PILOT 3: "Why Is That Sound So Loud?" (Sensory Science Story)
*   **Format:** 30-Second Micro-Story (Format 6 / Sensory POV).
*   **Characters:** Gappu & Dolly.
*   **Setting:** Brightly lit South Delhi Shopping Mall.
*   **The Creative Hypothesis:** Explaining the neurobiology of auditory hyperacusis visually will convert skeptical grandparents into supportive co-therapists.
*   **Exact Hook:** *"The room isn't loud to you. But to your child, it may feel like a thunderstorm."*
*   **Narrative Arc:**
    *   *0:00–0:08:* Visualizing a mall escalator. Visual transition: Lights swell into blinding neon; background chatter turns into roaring static.
    *   *0:08–0:20:* Dolly explains lack of auditory filtering: *"The brain's sound filter is wide open. Every shoe squeak, shopping cart rattle, and tube light hum arrives at full volume."*
    *   *0:20–0:27:* Gappu steps in, draws a calm blue translucent bubble around the child, handing over a squishy tactile stress ball. Harmony restored.
    *   *0:27–0:30:* Takeaway: *"Understand first. Support second."*
*   **Success Benchmark:** High educational authority, 80%+ completion rate on YouTube Shorts.
