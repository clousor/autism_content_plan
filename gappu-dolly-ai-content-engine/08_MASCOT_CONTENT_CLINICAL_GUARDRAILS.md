## 1. The Cardinal Mascot Rule: Mascots Do Not Diagnose

Under no circumstances may animated cartoon characters issue medical diagnoses or prescribe clinical therapies. Gappu and Dolly are **ambassadors of empathy and communication**, not diagnostic algorithms.

## 3. High-Risk Clinical Boundaries & Guardrails

### A. Hearing Checks Precede Sensory Assumptions
*   *Rule:* Never assert that because a child ignores spoken commands or startles at sounds, their hearing is confirmed normal.
*   *Clinical Requirement:* Any script discussing auditory differences must acknowledge that audiological evaluation (BERA / OAE) is the essential medical first step.

### B. Weighted Items & Physical Inputs
*   *Rule:* Never advise parents to purchase heavy blankets or vests indiscriminately.
*   *Clinical Requirement:* Scripts featuring weighted lap pads must clearly display on-screen: *"Weighted sensory items should be individually prescribed by a pediatric Occupational Therapist (typically 5–10% of body weight)."*

### C. AAC & Spoken Language
*   *Rule:* Never claim that Picture Exchange (PECS) or tablet AAC will "cure" speechlessness, nor that it will make speech unnecessary.
*   *Clinical Requirement:* Frame AAC accurately as a validated multi-modal communication bridge that reduces frustration and encourages functional exchange.
