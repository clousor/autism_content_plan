## 3. The 30-Day Decision Rules

*   **Scenario 1 (Animation Wins on Virality, Loses on Leads):** If Group B achieves 3x higher reach and shares, but Group A drives 80% of clinical consultations $\rightarrow$ **Adopt a 60/40 Split**: 60% Animated Reels at Top-of-Funnel to drive massive audience growth; 40% Dr. Atul human videos at Middle/Bottom-of-Funnel to convert consultations.
*   **Scenario 2 (Hybrid Wins Across All Metrics):** If Group C (Mascot Hook $\rightarrow$ Dr. Atul Solution) achieves top watch time AND high parent inquiries $\rightarrow$ **Scale the Hybrid Model** as the primary signature format across all channels.
*   **Scenario 3 (Animation Underperforms):** If Group B fails to achieve >50% hook stop rate due to cartoon aversion among serious parents $\rightarrow$ Restrict mascots strictly to children's educational storybooks, WhatsApp community stickers, and waiting-room screens, keeping main feeds doctor-led.
