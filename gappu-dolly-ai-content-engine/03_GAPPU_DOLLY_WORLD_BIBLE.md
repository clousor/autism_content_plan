## 2. Detailed Environmental Design Rules

### A. The Sensory Integration Gym (Centre)
*   **Aesthetic:** Warm Scandinavian birch wood accents, pastel foam padding, soft indirect cove lighting.
*   **Key Props:** Circular wooden platform swing with soft periwinkle ropes; large emerald green peanut therapy ball; dense royal blue crash mat; sensory fiber-optic bubble tube emitting gentle cyan glow.
*   **Sensory Atmosphere:** Quiet, safe, cushioned. No harsh fluorescent glare.

### B. The Indian Home Kitchen
*   **Aesthetic:** Modern urban Indian modular kitchen with traditional elements (stainless steel spice dabba, pressure cooker on hob, countertop mixer grinder).
*   **Sensory Dynamic:** The epicentre of household acoustic triggers. Whistling pressure cookers and roaring mixer grinders are used to demonstrate auditory sensitivity.

### C. The Inclusive Classroom
*   **Aesthetic:** Cheerful primary school classroom with pale yellow walls, wooden student desks arranged in small clusters, and large windows with sheer curtains.
*   **Key Prop:** A large, colorful **Visual Timetable** affixed to the front board using velcro picture cards showing the day's order: `[Prayer] -> [Math] -> [Recess] -> [Art] -> [Pack Bag]`.

### D. The Shopping Mall & Market
*   **Aesthetic:** Glossy polished floors, vibrant storefront signs, elevated glass railings, and a bustling crowd blurred gently into soft background bokeh.
*   **Sensory Dynamic:** Used to visualize sensory overload—lights twinkling intensely and background murmur swelling to demonstrate what a child feels during a meltdown.

## PART 3: CHILD DIGNITY & ANTI-STEREOTYPING RULES

1.  **Autism is NOT a Visual Caricature:** Fictional children in the universe (like Kabir) look like vibrant, beautiful, healthy children. They do not have distorted physical features or stereotypical "odd" appearances.
2.  **Focus on the Interaction, Not the Label:** Stories highlight the *environmental challenge* (e.g., the loud whistle or the unexpected schedule change) rather than medicalizing the child.
3.  **Presumption of Competence:** In every scene, whether Kabir is speaking, pointing, or quiet, all adults and mascots treat him with complete dignity, listening attentively and validating his choices.
