## 1. Mascot Pre-Publication Clinical Clearance Checklist

Every animated Reel must pass this 6-point clinical audit before publishing:

*   [ ] **1. No Diagnostic Claims:** The characters do NOT diagnose any condition or claim that a specific behavior "proves" autism.
*   [ ] **2. No Unrealistic Cures:** The script does NOT promise cures, rapid reversals, or 100% guarantees.
*   [ ] **3. Sensory Safety:** Any weighted items mentioned specify OT prescription and 5–10% body weight safety limits.
*   [ ] **4. Medical Gating:** Discussions on speech pauses reference hearing audiology (BERA) before sensory assumptions.
*   [ ] **5. Presumption of Competence:** The neurodivergent child is portrayed with complete human dignity and active agency.
*   [ ] **6. Ethical Framing:** The solution emphasizes parent co-regulation and environmental adaptation rather than forcing a child to mask.
