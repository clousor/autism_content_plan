## 1. Executive Synthesis: The Mascot Universe Imperative

Gappu and Dolly represent the emotional and educational heart of Autism Alliance. Rather than being passive brand illustrations, they form a **high-utility, emotionally transcendent animated content engine**. They translate dense neurodevelopmental neuroscience into intuitive, compassionate, visual parables that relieve maternal anxiety, educate Indian educators, and celebrate every child's unique way of connecting with the world.

## 2. Definitive Answers to the 20 Core Project Mandates

### 1. What exactly is Gappu's personality?
**Answer:** Gappu is the **"Thoughtful, Curious & Logical Mind"**. Embodying the ancient cognitive depth and memory of the elephant, Gappu is patient, observant, structured, and deeply reflective. He voices the honest questions parents hesitate to ask (*"Dolly, why does our friend look away when we speak?"*). He never behaves foolishly, never acts as a clown, and possesses a gentle, endearing situational wonder.

### 2. What exactly is Dolly's personality?
**Answer:** Dolly is the **"Empathetic, Intuitive & Compassionate Heart"**. Embodying the social warmth and emotional resonance of the dolphin, Dolly is perceptively attuned to the room's sensory energy. She feels emotional atmospheres instantly, guiding Gappu and the audience with melodic encouragement, soothing reassurance, and playful warmth.

### 3. How do their personalities complement each other?
**Answer:** They operate as a **Cognitive-Emotional Flywheel**:
*   Gappu asks the logical question $\rightarrow$ Dolly illuminates the sensory and emotional reality $\rightarrow$ Gappu designs the practical, respectful adaptation $\rightarrow$ Dolly celebrates the joyful connection.
*   Neither is superior to the other; genuine human understanding requires both the elephant's intellect and the dolphin's empathy.

### 4. What should they NEVER say or do?
**Answer:**
*   They must **NEVER issue a medical diagnosis** (*"You have autism"*, *"This means ASD"*).
*   They must **NEVER mock, ridicule, or laugh at a child's struggles**, meltdowns, or speech pauses.
*   They must **NEVER promise 30-day "cures" or guaranteed fixes**.
*   They must **NEVER advocate physical restraint, forced eye contact, or extinguishing safe stims**.

### 5. What animation style should Autism Alliance adopt?
**Answer:** **Soft 3D Stylized Family Animation** (High-end children's educational 3D with soft subsurface scattering, velvet clay matte skin textures, and warm diffused lighting). This style is emotionally expressive, visually premium, child-friendly, parent-resonant, and completely avoids the creepy uncanny valley of hyper-realistic CGI.
