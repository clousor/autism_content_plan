## 2. 9:16 Vertical Safe Zone Architecture (1080 × 1920)

To prevent platform UI elements (username, captions, like buttons, audio tickers) from obscuring critical visual storytelling, editors must observe strict safe zones:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                1080 × 1920 VERTICAL REELS SAFE ZONE MAP                 │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [TOP UNSAFE ZONE — 0px to 250px]                                        │
│ • Reserved for Instagram camera notch, search bar, and app headers.     │
│ • ONLY subtle Autism Alliance logo placed here (Top-Right: 120px wide). │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                         │
│ [PRIMARY VISUAL CANVAS — 250px to 1450px]                               │
│ • All character faces, emotional eyes, trunks, and flippers live here.  │
│ • Hook Banner: Placed at Y=320px to Y=460px (Center).                  │
│ • Supporting Dialogue Subtitles: Placed at Y=1250px to Y=1420px.        │
│ • Horizontal Margin: Keep characters within central 920px (80px inset).│
│                                                                         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [BOTTOM UNSAFE ZONE — 1450px to 1920px]                                 │
│ • Blocked by account username, audio track title, and caption text.     │
│ • ZERO critical animation or text may appear in this bottom 470px zone! │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Typography & Subtitle System

*   **Hook Font (Upper Banner):** `Montserrat Black` or `Poppins ExtraBold` (Uppercase, 48pt).
    *   *Styling:* Bold black text encased in a rounded `#FFD21E` yellow capsule with a soft 8px drop shadow.
*   **Dialogue Subtitles (Bottom Area):** `Inter Bold` or `Nunito ExtraBold` (Mixed Case, 42pt).
    *   *Styling:* Pure `#FFFFFF` white letters with a solid 3px `#071A2F` deep navy contour stroke. Maximum 4–5 words per display burst to ensure effortless reading speed.
*   **Series Label Badge:** Pinned at Y=260px (Top Left):
    *   `[ GAPPU & DOLLY • DIFFERENT WAYS TO CONNECT ]` (18pt, semi-transparent frosted navy glass).

## 4. The 5 Repeatable Thumbnail Templates

1.  **Format T1: Character Wonder + Big Question:** Gappu touching his trunk to cheek looking upward at large 3-word bold question (*"WHY LOOK AWAY?"*).
2.  **Format T2: The Visual Contrast (Wrong vs Right):** Split vertical thumbnail; left side shows red 'X' over loud mixer; right side shows green checkmark with cozy headphones.
3.  **Format T3: Gappu & Dolly Duo Embrace:** Canonical hugging shot with a warm, heart-melting headline (*"THEY UNDERSTAND."*).
4.  **Format T4: The Myth Buster Stamp:** Gappu holding a parchment with a huge angled red distressed stamp: `MYTH: BOYS TALK LATE`.
5.  **Format T5: Interactive "What Would You Do?":** Kabir sitting on swing looking hesitant with two multiple-choice pill buttons: `A) PUSH` or `B) WAIT`.
