## 2. Detailed Deliverable Specifications by Phase

### PHASE 1: NOW (Days 1–60 — Brand Foundation & Social Proof)
1.  **Waiting Room Ambient Screens:** A continuous 3-minute, sound-optional animated video loop playing on LCD screens in Delhi East of Kailash, Faridabad Crown Plaza, and Ludhiana Kochar Market Road reception lounges. Shows Gappu and Dolly demonstrating calm breathing and welcoming visiting families.
2.  **WhatsApp Sticker Pack (20 Animated Stickers):** High-utility stickers for parent support groups:
    *   *Gappu deep breath:* "Take it one moment at a time!"
    *   *Dolly water splash:* "Victory! Micro-milestone achieved!"
    *   *Gappu with blanket:* "Sensory calm time."
3.  **Physical Centre Standees:** 5-foot die-cut acrylic standees at clinic entrances featuring Gappu and Dolly holding a speech bubble: *"Welcome to our happy space!"* (Parents love photographing their children with the mascots).

---

### PHASE 2: NEXT (Months 3–6 — Educational Literature & Schools)
1.  **The First Official Storybook: *"Gappu & Dolly and the Loud Kitchen"*:**
    *   *Format:* 24-page full-color children’s picture book in Hindi & English.
    *   *Plot:* Gappu and Dolly help Kabir understand his auditory sensitivity and design a calm sensory toolkit.
    *   *Distribution:* Gifted to every newly enrolled child in the Autism Alliance 3-Month Parent Training Program (PTP) and sold on Amazon/website.
2.  **CBSE / ICSE School Sensitization Pack:**
    *   A 3-minute animated explainer licensed free of charge to progressive partner schools (Apeejay, Bluebells, Tagore Int.) to be screened during morning assemblies for World Autism Month.

---

### PHASE 3: LATER (Months 6–12 — Commercial IP & National Advocacy)
1.  **Therapeutic Sensory Merchandise:**
    *   *The "Weighted Gappu" Lap Plushie:* A certified 1.5kg sensory lap plush elephant filled with non-toxic ceramic micro-beads, designed for restless children during homework.
    *   *The "Dolly Water Bottle":* Ergonomic, textured silicon sensory water bottles with visual gulp trackers.
2.  **National Pediatric Waiting Room Comic Book:**
    *   Distributed across 500+ private pediatric clinics and hospital OPDs across North India, replacing dry medical pamphlets with a warm, story-driven comic that demystifies early childhood milestone delays.
