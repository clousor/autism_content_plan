# Autism Alliance — Gappu & Dolly AI Animated Content Universe

Welcome to the **Gappu & Dolly AI Content Engine** repository. This directory contains the complete, production-ready intellectual property (IP) architecture, 60-day publishing calendar, AI animation generation prompts, 30 scene-by-scene scripted video reels, and clinical safety systems for Autism Alliance's beloved mascots: **Gappu (The Elephant)** and **Dolly (The Dolphin)**.
