## 4. Core Services & Offerings `[VERIFIED]`
1.  **Diagnostic & Developmental Assessment:** Clinical psychological assessments, standardized screening tools, developmental delay evaluations.
2.  **Occupational Therapy (OT) & Sensory Integration (SI):** Motor skills, sensory processing, regulation, everyday self-help tasks.
3.  **Speech & Language Therapy:** Expressive/receptive language, articulation, non-verbal communication, AAC (Alternative & Augmentative Communication), joint attention.
4.  **Special Education & School Readiness:** Academic readiness, cognitive skills, visual schedules, IEP (Individualized Education Program) implementation.
5.  **Behavior Therapy (ABA-based):** Functional communication training, positive behavior support, meltdown mitigation, ABC analysis.
6.  **Parent Training Program (PTP):** Proprietary structured coaching empowering parents to implement therapeutic strategies at home.
7.  **Diet & Nutrition Support:** Addressing food selectivity, gut-brain sensitivities, texture aversion, nutritional balance.
8.  **Care Coordination & Family Support:** Long-term roadmap guidance, school accommodation advisory (under RTE/RPwD Acts).

## 5. Target Audience Segments `[VERIFIED & HYPOTHESIS]`
1.  **Audience A — Early Noticers (Anxious Questioners):** Parents of children aged 12 months to 4 years noticing speech delay, lack of eye contact, no name response, toe walking, or flapping. Seeking answers without panic.

5.  **Audience E — Pediatricians & Healthcare Referrals:** Family physicians and pediatricians seeking a trusted, RCI-registered multidisciplinary referral centre.

## 7. Absolute Content & Clinical Restrictions `[MANDATORY COMPLIANCE]`
1.  **NO Cure Claims:** Never claim autism can be "cured" or "reversed". Use "developmental support", "skill building", "communication progress".
2.  **NO Guaranteed Timelines:** Never promise "speech in 30 days" or "100% meltdown elimination".
3.  **NO Fear-Based Hooks:** Avoid sensational hooks like *"Is your child doomed if they don't speak by age 2?"*
4.  **NO Unconsented Child Faces / Private Distress:** Never film meltdowns, distress, diagnostic assessment scoring sheets, or identifiable child profiles without explicit written consent. Focus on therapist hands, toys, setups, and simulations.
5.  **NO Remote Diagnosis in DMs/Comments:** Always state that online guidance is educational and cannot replace an in-person clinical assessment by an RCI-registered professional.
