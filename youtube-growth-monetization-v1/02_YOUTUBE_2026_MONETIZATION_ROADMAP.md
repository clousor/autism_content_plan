### A. YouTube Health Shelf & Creator Accreditation (India)
YouTube has rolled out accredited health information shelves in India to elevate credible medical professionals over unverified advice.
*   **Accreditation Qualification:** Dr. Atul Madaan holds a recognized clinical psychology registration under the **Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI CRR No. A56122)**, along with an MPhil in Clinical Psychology and PhD in Psychology.
*   **Actionable Implementation:** Apply through YouTube Studio under the **YouTube Health Creator Program**. Once accredited, Dr. Atul's videos receive:
    1.  The official **"From an accredited clinical psychologist"** information panel beneath videos.
    2.  Priority algorithmic placement on high-stakes medical search queries (*"autism diagnosis India"*, *"early autism signs"*, *"speech delay therapy"*).

### B. Medical Misinformation & Advertiser-Friendly Guidelines
*   **Strictly Prohibited:** Claiming autism can be "reversed in 30 days", endorsing hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) or chemical chelation for autism, or linking autism to childhood vaccines.
*   **Advertiser-Friendly Rating:** Child behavioral distress (e.g., self-harm, head banging) must be discussed with clinical professionalism, using anatomical diagrams or verbal explanations rather than sensationalized, graphic video clips of children crying, which risks demonetization (Yellow Dollar Icon).

### C. Synthetic & AI Media Disclosure (Altered Content Rules)
*   **The 2026 YouTube Studio Toggle:** Creators must disclose when synthetic media depicts realistic humans saying or doing things they did not do.
*   **Gappu & Dolly Protocol:** Our animated mascots are **stylized, storybook 3D cartoon characters**. Under YouTube's published policy guidelines, stylized 2D/3D cartoon animation is recognizable as non-realistic by average viewers and does not require synthetic media disclosure tags, provided no real person's likeness or cloned doctor voice is used deceptively.
