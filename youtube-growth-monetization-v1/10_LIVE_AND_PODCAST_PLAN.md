## 3. Live Chat Clinical Triage & Moderation Rules

During a live stream with 300+ concurrent parents, unmoderated chat becomes chaotic with desperate medical queries. A dedicated **Clinical Content Moderator** (Junior Psychologist or Senior Coordinator) manages chat triage via StreamYard:

### The 3 Chat Filters:
1.  **Standard Parent Query Format (Pinned in Chat):**
    *   *Requirement:* Viewers are instructed to post in format: `[Child Age] + [Core Symptom] + [How Long]`.
    *   *Example:* `"Boy, 2.5 years, no name response, spinning car wheels for 6 months."`
2.  **Medical Emergency / Medication Filter (Red Tier):**
    *   *Rule:* Any query asking *"Which medicine will cure my child?"* or describing severe acute crisis is immediately handled with standard moderator copy: *"Dr. Atul cannot prescribe allopathic medication on a public live stream. Please consult your developmental pediatrician or call our helpline for 1-on-1 clinical evaluation."*
3.  **Super Chat Prioritization (Stage 2 Monetization):**
    *   Super Chats (₹100 to ₹1,000) are flagged and brought onto the live broadcast screen as an on-screen banner for direct doctor consultation.
