## 3. The 4 Master Title Engineering Formulas

Titles must balance **Search Query Match (SEO)** with **Click Triggers (Curiosity & Empathy)**. We forbid clickbait that breaks trust; our titles make bold promises that the video completely fulfills.

### FORMULA 1: The Search + Hinglish Question Formula (Evergreen SEO)
*   *Structure:* `[English Clinical Search Keyword] : [Hinglish Parent Question] | Dr. Atul Madaan`
*   *Example:* `Speech Delay vs Autism: Farak Kaise Pehchaanein? | Complete Guide by Dr. Atul Madaan`
*   *Why It Works:* Captures high-intent Google/YouTube searchers while reassuring Hindi-speaking parents that the video is conversational and accessible.

### FORMULA 2: The Myth & Pattern-Interrupt Formula (Browse & Suggested)
*   *Structure:* `[Common Cultural Myth / Mistake] : Why [Surprising Clinical Reality]`
*   *Example:* `Why Boys Don't Just "Talk Late": The Dangerous Myth Delaying Autism Therapy`
*   *Why It Works:* Challenges deep-rooted folklore (*"ladke der se bolte hain"*), compelling parents and relatives to click to resolve cognitive dissonance.

### FORMULA 3: The Age & Urgent Solution Formula (Parent Pain Point)
*   *Structure:* `[Child Age / Problem] : [Concrete Actionable Framework]`
*   *Example:* `2-Year-Old Not Responding to Name? 3 Clinical Checks You Can Do at Home`
*   *Why It Works:* Targets parents by exact child age, giving an immediate, low-friction promise (*"at home"*).

### FORMULA 4: The Empathy & Guilt-Relief Formula (High Social Sharing)
*   *Structure:* `To the Parent [Struggling with Emotion] : [Reassuring Clinical Truth]`
*   *Example:* `Did Screen Time Cause Your Child's Autism? The Truth Every Mother Needs to Hear`
*   *Why It Works:* Directly addresses maternal guilt, driving massive comment engagement and peer sharing on WhatsApp.

### Algorithmic Evaluation Protocol:
1.  **Launch Window (Day 1 to Day 5):** Allow YouTube Studio to rotate all 3 variations across search, browse, and suggested impressions.
2.  **Decision Metric:** YouTube automatically selects the winner based on **Watch Time Share** (not just raw clicks, ensuring the thumbnail doesn't create click-regret).
3.  **Post-Test Action:** Once YouTube locks the winner, update the thumbnail across all related Shorts and community posts.
