## 1. Official YouTube / Google Policy Sources (2026 Standards)

### A. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) & Monetization Thresholds
1.  **YouTube Partner Program Overview & Minimum Eligibility Criteria:**
    *   *URL:* `https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851`
    *   *Core Thresholds (Standard Tier / Full Ad Revenue Sharing):*
        *   Minimum 1,000 Subscribers, AND
        *   4,000 valid public watch hours on long-form videos in the last 12 months, OR
        *   10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days.
        *   Active 2-Step Verification on Google Account.
        *   Zero active Community Guidelines strikes.
        *   Linked active Google AdSense account.
    *   *Early Access Tier (Fan Funding & Shopping):*
        *   Minimum 500 Subscribers.
        *   3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days.
        *   3,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months, OR 3 million valid Shorts views in the last 90 days.
        *   Unlocks: Channel Memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, and YouTube Shopping.

2.  **Shorts Monetization & Ad Revenue Sharing Model:**
    *   *URL:* `https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220`
    *   *Mechanics:* Revenue from ads between Shorts in the Shorts Feed is pooled every month. Creator Pool funds are allocated based on each creator's share of total views, with creators retaining **45% of allocated revenue** regardless of whether commercial music is used.

### B. YouTube Health & Medical Information Quality Principles
1.  **YouTube Health Accreditation for Health Creators in India:**
    *   *URL:* `https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12374976`
    *   *Policy Mandate:* YouTube partners with the National Academy of Medicine (NAM) and public health organizations to highlight authoritative health sources. In India, certified clinical psychologists (RCI registered), pediatricians, and licensed healthcare institutions can apply for the **Health Information Shelf** and **Health Content Badges**.
    *   *Clinical Application:* Dr. Atul Madaan's credentials (PhD Psychology, MPhil Clinical Psychology, RCI CRR No. A56122) qualify the channel for accredited health authority status, boosting search placement for health queries.

2.  **Medical Misinformation Policy:**
    *   *URL:* `https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9891785`
    *   *Specific Autism Rules:* YouTube strictly bans content that claims autism can be "cured", content promoting unproven, dangerous treatments (e.g., chlorine dioxide, hyperbaric oxygen therapy for autism, chelation therapy), or claiming that approved childhood vaccines cause autism.
    *   *Editorial Rule:* All Autism Alliance content adheres to evidence-based neurodevelopmental standards and parent empowerment.

### C. AI-Generated, Altered & Synthetic Content Disclosure
1.  **Transparency & Synthetic Media Disclosure Policy (2024–2026):**
    *   *URL:* `https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491`
    *   *Requirement:* Creators must select the "Altered content" checkbox in YouTube Studio when publishing realistic synthetic media (e.g., realistic digital humans, voice cloning of real persons, or realistic simulated events).
    *   *Mascot Animation Exemption:* Stylized 3D cartoon characters (like Gappu the Elephant and Dolly the Dolphin) are clearly animated and non-realistic; they do not trigger mandatory "altered content" warning badges unless realistic human likenesses or cloned medical clinician voices are generated without disclosure.

### D. Reused, Repetitious & Advertiser-Friendly Guidelines
1.  **Reused Content & Repetitious Content Policy:**
    *   *URL:* `https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392`
    *   *Enforcement:* Channels that mass-produce low-effort, templated, automated voiceover slideshows without original commentary or human persona are flagged as "Repetitious Content" and demonetized.
    *   *Strategic Mitigation:* The channel is anchored by Dr. Atul Madaan's authentic clinical persona, expert guest therapists, and proprietary original animation, ensuring 100% compliance with original educational value standards.

2.  **Advertiser-Friendly Content Guidelines (Ad-Friendly Rating):**
    *   *URL:* `https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278`
    *   *Guidelines:* Sensitive topics regarding child distress, self-harm, or severe aggression must be framed educationally and therapeutically without graphic dramatization or shock imagery to maintain green monetization status.
