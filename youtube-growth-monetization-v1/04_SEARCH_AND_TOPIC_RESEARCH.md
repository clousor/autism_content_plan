1.  **Speech Delay vs Autism:**
    *   *Search Intent:* High-anxiety search query by parents of 18–36 month olds.
    *   *Target Keyword:* `speech delay vs autism in hindi`, `bachha bolta nahi hai kya karein`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *Speech Delay vs Autism: 4 Clinical Observations Parents Can Check at Home*.
2.  **Early Intervention Timelines:**
    *   *Search Intent:* Questions about the 0–3 neuroplasticity window.
    *   *Target Keyword:* `early intervention autism in india`, `autism early treatment benefits`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *The Golden Age Window (18M–4Y): Why Waiting Costs 3 Years of Progress*.
3.  **Name Response & Eye Contact:**
    *   *Search Intent:* Specific milestone delays causing panic.
    *   *Target Keyword:* `child not responding to name`, `eye contact improve kaise karein`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *Why Autistic Children Avoid Eye Contact (And Why Forcing It Causes Harm)*.
4.  **Autism vs ADHD Differential:**
    *   *Search Intent:* Confusion over overlapping symptoms (inattention, hyperactivity).
    *   *Target Keyword:* `autism vs adhd farak hindi`, `hyperactive child vs autism`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *Autism or ADHD? How Clinical Psychologists Tell Them Apart*.
5.  **Occupational Therapy (OT) Decoded:**
    *   *Search Intent:* Parents who received an OT referral and have no idea what it entails.
    *   *Target Keyword:* `what is occupational therapy for autism`, `ot therapy activities at home`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *Inside an Autism Sensory Gym: Why Swings and Crash Pads are Serious Therapy*.
6.  **Speech-Language Therapy Milestones:**
    *   *Search Intent:* Parents seeking speech exercises for non-verbal toddlers.
    *   *Target Keyword:* `speech therapy exercises for toddlers at home`, `autism speech therapy hindi`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *Why Flashcards Won't Teach Your Child to Speak: The 3 Speech Therapy Pre-Requisites*.
7.  **Selective Eating & Food Textures:**
    *   *Search Intent:* Extreme picky eating and gagging on mixed foods.
    *   *Target Keyword:* `autism picky eating solutions`, `bachha khana nahi chabata`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *Sensory Food Refusal: 5 Steps to Expand Your Child's Diet Without Tears*.
8.  **Toilet Training & Interoception:**
    *   *Search Intent:* Desperate parents of 4–7 year olds struggling with potty training.
    *   *Target Keyword:* `autism potty training guide hindi`, `toilet training special needs child`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *The 8th Sense (Interoception): Why Standard Potty Training Fails for Autistic Kids*.
9.  **Formal Diagnostic Assessment:**
    *   *Search Intent:* Parents preparing for a clinical evaluation.
    *   *Target Keyword:* `autism test for child in india`, `cars 2 test autism cost`.
    *   *Long-Form Angle:* *What Happens During a Real Autism Clinical Assessment: Step-by-Step Breakdown*.
10. **Special Education & IEPs:**

    *   *Target Keyword:* `special education for autism schools in delhi`, `iep meaning in hindi`.
