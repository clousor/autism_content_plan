## 2. Content Inventory & Format Performance Audit

### A. Long-Form Video Performance Analysis
Long-form video is the **backbone of trust** for Dr. Atul Madaan. Recent long-form uploads (averaging 12 to 24 minutes) demonstrate consistent view velocity:

*   **Breakout / High-Performing Long-Form Topics:**
    1.  *Faculty Development Program: Why Autistic Children Show Self-Hitting, Tapping/Scratching Behaviour* $\rightarrow$ **90,000+ views** (Deep clinical explanation of intense parent pain points).
    2.  *Autism में Parents का Role: Behaviour, Screen Time और Daily Skills को कैसे Manage करें?* $\rightarrow$ **12,000 views** (Empowerment thesis resonates strongly).
    3.  *Autism में Tapping Behaviour क्यों होता है? Sensory Need या Attention?* $\rightarrow$ **6,600 views** (Deconstructs specific repetitive motor actions).
    4.  *Autism Parenting: How to Build the Right Structure at Home* $\rightarrow$ **4,400 views** (Practical utility).
    5.  *Child Self-Hitting & Head Banging: Sensory Issue or Behaviour?* $\rightarrow$ **4,100 views** (Urgent crisis management).
    6.  *Autism: How to Improve Communication, Schooling & Socialization | Parent Q&A* $\rightarrow$ **3,200 views** (Broad multi-topic authority).
    7.  *Why Early Intervention Matters in Autism | Parent Training, Echolalia & Speech Explained* $\rightarrow$ **2,800 views** in 5 days (Strong velocity).

*   **Underperforming Long-Form Topics:**
    1.  *Unpackaged / Generic Live Stream Replays:* Videos titled simply *"Live Autism Q&A with Dr. Atul Madaan | Autism Alliance Faridabad"* without clear question stamps achieve only **222 views**.
    2.  *Abstract Discussion Without Clear Parent Problem:* Videos focusing broadly on healthcare admin rather than immediate home challenges hover below 1,000 views.

### B. YouTube Shorts Performance Audit
Shorts currently represent the **largest under-leveraged growth gap** on the channel:
*   **Current Uploads:** Recent Shorts consist mostly of cropped podcast snippets (e.g. from `#theramanshow`) or unedited live stream excerpts:
    *   *Can TV or Sweets Cause Autism?* $\rightarrow$ **167 views** (1 day ago).
    *   *Autism Myth: Every Child With Speech Delay Has Autism* $\rightarrow$ **315 views**.
    *   *Why Treatment Fails: Are You Following the Doctor's Advice?* $\rightarrow$ **789 views**.
    *   *Screen Time Is Like a Drug for Children?* $\rightarrow$ **724 views**.
    *   *Your Child Knows ABCD But Can't Say "Mamma"?* $\rightarrow$ **1,900 views** (Highest performing Short).
*   **Audit Diagnosis:**
    *   Shorts are treated as an afterthought rather than a deliberate discovery machine.
    *   Crucially, **the native YouTube Studio "Related Video" link is not systematically attached**, meaning thousands of prospective parents who see a Short are never guided to Dr. Atul's full 20-minute masterclass.
    *   Thumbnails for Shorts are auto-selected frames rather than bold, high-contrast title cards.

---

### C. Live Streams & Podcasts
*   **Live Streams:** Dr. Atul conducts occasional YouTube Live Q&As. However, they lack advance scheduling (Community tab posts, waiting room count-downs), and live chats are addressed without on-screen question overlays. Post-live, the recordings are published without chapter timestamps, limiting their evergreen replay value.
*   **Podcasts / Guest Interviews:** Collaborations like `#theramanshow` show great chemistry and high authority, proving Dr. Atul's interview charisma. However, these clips are scattered across the channel without a dedicated "Dr. Atul Conversations" playlist hub.
