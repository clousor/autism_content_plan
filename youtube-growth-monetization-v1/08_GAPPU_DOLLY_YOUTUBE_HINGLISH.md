## PART 1: AUDIT OF PREVIOUS SCRIPTS FOR YOUTUBE VIABILITY

1.  **Linguistic Audit:** Earlier conceptual drafts in English sounded too academic for average Indian parents searching YouTube. Spoken lines like *'Auditory hyperacusis impairs processing'* have been converted into natural living room Hinglish: *'Mixer grinder ka shor ek thunderstorm jaise lagta hai!'*
2.  **Maturity Check:** Mascots must never sound like screechy nursery rhyme characters. Gappu speaks with thoughtful, gentle boy wonder (7–8yo tenor), while Dolly speaks with warm, soothing, maternal girl reassurance (8–9yo soprano).
3.  **The Funnel Link:** Every animated Short ends with an explicit bridge directing viewers to Dr. Atul Madaan's corresponding full-length masterclass on YouTube.
